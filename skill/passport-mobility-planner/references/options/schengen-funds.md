# Schengen reference amounts

Minimum means of subsistence each Schengen state publishes for short stays.

- Austria: On a case-by-case basis. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Belgium: EUR 45 per day if staying with a private individual; EUR 95 per day if staying at a hotel. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Bulgaria: EUR 50 per day; EUR 500 per stay. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Croatia: EUR 70 per day; EUR 30 per day with a certified letter of guarantee from a Croatian person or company, a tourist booking or a similar document. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Cyprus: On a case-by-case basis. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Czechia: Stays up to 30 days: CZK 1,565 per day (half the subsistence minimum of CZK 3,130). Stays over 30 days: CZK 46,950 (15 times the subsistence minimum), plus CZK 6,260 for each whole month of stay. Under 18s prove half these amounts. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Denmark: DKK 350 per day. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Estonia: EUR 70 per day. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Finland: On a case-by-case basis; EUR 50 per day, in addition to funds or tickets for departure and accommodation during the stay. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- France: EUR 65 per day; EUR 32.50 with proof of accommodation; EUR 120 without proof of accommodation. If a hotel is booked for part of the stay: EUR 65 for the booked period and EUR 120 for the rest. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Germany: EUR 45 per day. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Greece: EUR 50 per day; EUR 25 per day for minors; EUR 300 for a stay of up to 5 days. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Hungary: EUR 40 per day. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Iceland: ISK 8,000 per day; ISK 40,000 for each entry. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Italy: EUR 269.60 fixed sum for 1 to 5 days overall. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Latvia: EUR 14 per day; no less than the minimum monthly wage (EUR 700) for stays over 30 days. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Liechtenstein: Around CHF 100 per day; around CHF 30 per day for students with a valid student ID. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Lithuania: EUR 50 per day. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Luxembourg: EUR 67 per day. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Malta: EUR 48 per day. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Netherlands: EUR 55 per day. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Norway: On a case-by-case basis; NOK 300 per day with hosted or prepaid accommodation; NOK 1,300 per day without proof of accommodation; with more than one traveller, at least NOK 1,000 per person per day. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Poland: PLN 300 for stays of up to 4 days; PLN 75 per day for longer stays. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Portugal: EUR 75 for each entry; EUR 40 for each day in the country. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Romania: EUR 50 per day, but no less than EUR 500 for the whole stay, or equivalent; EUR 30 per day for third-country nationals under the invitation procedure. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Slovakia: EUR 56 per day. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Slovenia: EUR 70 per day; EUR 35 per day for minors accompanied by their parents. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Spain: 10% of the gross national minimum wage (EUR 122.10) per day; 90% of the gross national minimum wage (EUR 1,098.90) as the minimum amount available. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Sweden: SEK 700 per day. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
- Switzerland: CHF 100 per day; CHF 30 per day for students. Source: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9bf9a98f-3bd1-4e14-9b39-c9087a08a32f_en?filename=Reference_amounts_table.pdf (European Commission, Directorate-General for Migration and Home Affairs), checked 07 Nov 2026
