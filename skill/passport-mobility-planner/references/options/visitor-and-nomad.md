## Australia

### Electronic Travel Authority (subclass 601) - au-eta-601

- Document: electronic travel authorisation
- Purpose: tourism, business visitor activities
- Fee: AUD 20 service charge for the Australian ETA app (no visa application charge)
- Waiting time: Usually notified immediately
- Validity: 12 months from grant, or until the passport expires if sooner; multiple entries
- Stay: Up to 3 months each time
- How to apply: Through the Australian ETA app, while outside Australia
- Eligibility: Holders of an eligible passport who are outside Australia
- For ranking: fee about USD 13, 0 days
- Required documents: passport, application-form
- Only for nationals of: Andorra, Austria, Belgium, Brunei Darussalam, Canada, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hong Kong, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, Malaysia, Malta, Monaco, Norway, Portugal, San Marino, Singapore, South Korea, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Taiwan, Netherlands, United Kingdom, United States, Holy See (Vatican City State)
- Notes: Taiwan passports qualify only if they are not official or diplomatic passports. UK passports qualify as British Citizen or British National (Overseas).
- Source: https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/electronic-travel-authority-601 (Department of Home Affairs, Australian Government)

## Canada

### Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA) - ca-eta

- Document: electronic travel authorisation
- Purpose: visits and transit by air
- Fee: CAD 7
- Waiting time: Most approved within minutes; some can take several days
- Validity: Up to five years, or until the passport expires if sooner
- Stay: Usually up to six months at a time
- How to apply: Online on the Government of Canada eTA form before booking the flight
- Eligibility: Visa-exempt foreign nationals flying to, or transiting through, a Canadian airport
- For ranking: fee about USD 5, waiting days unknown
- Required documents: passport, application-form
- Notes: Not needed for arrivals by land or sea. The fee is from the IRCC fee list: https://ircc.canada.ca/english/information/fees/fees.asp
- Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/eta/facts.html (Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC))

## Indonesia

### E33 second home visa - id-e33-second-home-visa

- Document: visa
- Purpose: long stay for people who deposit funds or buy property in Indonesia
- Fee: IDR 13,000,000 (up to 5 years)
- Validity: Must be used to enter within 90 days of issue
- Stay: Up to 5 years, extendable
- How to apply: Online on the official Indonesia eVisa website; the portal marks this visa as applied through a guarantor
- Eligibility: Foreigners who will deposit funds in a state-owned Indonesian bank or buy property in Indonesia
- Proof of funds: Within 90 days of arrival: deposit USD 130,000 in a state-owned bank account in your name, or buy property such as an apartment worth USD 1,000,000
- For ranking: fee about USD 800, waiting days unknown
- Required documents: passport, passport-photo, cv, flight-booking, bank-statements, other
- Notes: An e-ITAS stay permit and re-entry permit are issued on arrival if conditions are met. Commitments must be reported within 90 days of entry. Passport must be valid at least 6 months.
- Source: https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/web/visa-selection (Directorate General of Immigration, Ministry of Immigration and Corrections, Republic of Indonesia)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### E33G remote worker visa - id-e33g-remote-worker-visa

- Document: visa
- Purpose: remote work for a company outside Indonesia
- Fee: IDR 7,000,000 (1 year)
- Validity: Must be used to enter within 90 days of issue
- Stay: Up to 1 year
- How to apply: Online on the official Indonesia eVisa website
- Eligibility: Foreigners employed under contract by a company established outside Indonesia
- Proof of funds: Personal bank statement with at least USD 2,000 (or equivalent) over the last 3 months, and income of at least USD 60,000 a year
- For ranking: fee about USD 430, waiting days unknown
- Required documents: passport, bank-statements, passport-photo, cv, flight-booking, proof-of-income, employment-contract
- Notes: Passport must be valid at least 6 months. Holders may not sell goods or services or take paid work from people or companies in Indonesia. The portal lists the cost as 7,000,000 without a currency label; the other visas on the portal are priced in IDR.
- Source: https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/web/visa-selection (Directorate General of Immigration, Ministry of Immigration and Corrections, Republic of Indonesia)

## Malaysia

### DE Rantau Nomad Pass (Professional Visit Pass) - my-de-rantau-nomad-pass

- Document: visa
- Purpose: remote work as a digital nomad
- Fee: MYR 1,080 processing fee per applicant (MYR 540 per dependant), plus immigration pass fee MYR 90 per 3 months or MYR 360 per year
- Waiting time: 6 to 8 weeks after a complete application is received; the e-Pass is usually issued within 1 week of endorsement
- Validity: Initial pass of 3 to 12 months, renewable up to three times for 12 months each (maximum 48 months)
- Stay: 3 to 12 months at first
- How to apply: Online through the MDEC DE Rantau portal; endorsement is completed in Malaysia within 1 month of entry
- Eligibility: Tech talent (IT and digital roles) earning at least USD 36,000 a year, or listed non-tech roles earning at least USD 60,000 a year, working for foreign companies or clients
- Proof of funds: Annual income of at least USD 36,000 (tech) or USD 60,000 (non-tech), shown with 3 months of payslips or invoices and matching bank statements
- For ranking: fee about USD 240, 56 days
- Required documents: passport, application-form, employment-contract, payslips, bank-statements, employment-letter, health-insurance
- Notes: New applications are closed from 1 to 30 November 2026 and reopen on 1 December 2026 under an enhanced framework. Passport must have more than 14 months validity when applying. The processing fee is not refunded. Multiple-entry visa charges vary by nationality. Fees and rules are from the MDEC FAQ, version 11 (1 November 2026): https://www.mdec.my/api/media/file/DE-Rantau_Pass_FAQ_Version-11_%2029102026.pdf
- Source: https://www.mdec.my/md-programmes/digital-nomad-pass (Malaysia Digital Economy Corporation (MDEC))

## New Zealand

- Document: electronic travel authorisation
- Purpose: visits, transit through Auckland Airport
- Fee: From NZD 17
- Waiting time: Allow 72 hours
- Validity: 2 years for travellers (5 years for crew); multiple trips

### ESTA (Electronic System for Travel Authorization) - us-esta

- Document: electronic travel authorisation
- Purpose: tourism, business, transit under the Visa Waiver Program
- Fee: USD 40.62
- Validity: Usually two years, or until the passport expires if sooner
- Stay: 90 days or less per visit under the Visa Waiver Program
- How to apply: Online through the official ESTA website or the ESTA Mobile app before travel
- Eligibility: Citizens of Visa Waiver Program countries travelling for 90 days or less who hold an electronic passport
- For ranking: fee about USD 41, waiting days unknown
- Required documents: passport, application-form
- Notes: The USD 40.62 fee for fiscal year 2027 is set by Federal Register notice 2026-20185 (published 1 October 2026, in force 15 days later): USD 17 processing plus USD 10.62 and USD 13 in other charges. A refused application is charged USD 10.62. CBP gives no processing time on the FAQ page. Notice: https://www.federalregister.gov/d/2026-20185
- Source: https://www.cbp.gov/travel/international-visitors/esta/frequently-asked-questions-about-visa-waiver-program-vwp-and-electronic-system-travel (U.S. Customs and Border Protection)
