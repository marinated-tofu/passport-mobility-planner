## Steps in order

### 1. Get a tax number (NIF) - pt-nif

- Category: tax-number
- Who: Foreign nationals who will work, rent, open a bank account or pay taxes in Portugal, resident or not
- Fee: Free
- How: Book an appointment at a Finanças office through the tax authority helpline (217 206 707) and bring your passport with your entry visa, or your residence document. If you live outside the EU/EEA you can apply online through e-balcão via a representative.
- Notes: Non-residents need a tax representative who lives in Portugal. Foreign residents with a Portuguese address can ask for the NIF, NISS and SNS number together through a single gov.pt service. Your NIF, NISS and SNS numbers can be checked later in the gov.pt reserved area, the gov.pt app or at an Espaço Cidadão, and they do not expire.
- Needs: passport, visa, residence-permit
- Source: https://www.gov.pt/guias/como-pedir-o-nif-e-o-niss-para-pessoas-estrangeiras-em-portugal (Government of Portugal (gov.pt) / Autoridade Tributária e Aduaneira)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### 2. Apply for a residence permit with AIMA - pt-aima-residence-permit

- Category: residence-registration
- Who: Non-EU nationals who entered Portugal on a residence visa and plan to stay
- Fee: €133.00 reception and analysis plus €114.30 for grant of a temporary permit (AIMA fee table, Portaria 307/2023); the same table lists a lower column of €99.80 and €85.80
- How: Book an appointment with AIMA (non-CPLP residence-visa holders book online at contactenos.aima.gov.pt, uploading copies of passport and visa), then submit signed form Modelo 1 and your documents in person at a Loja AIMA.
- Notes: Accommodation proof is a sworn declaration of your address plus a land registry certificate (owners) or a landlord or host declaration (tenants). Also bring proof of tax and social security registration where applicable, and kinship proof for family. You must not have a conviction for a crime punishable by more than one year. A temporary permit lasts 2 years and renews for successive 3-year periods. Fee table: https://aima.gov.pt/documents/tabela-de-taxas-e-demais-encargos-a-cobrar-pelos-procedimentos-administrativos.pdf
- Needs: passport, visa, proof-of-income, accommodation-proof, health-insurance, application-form
- Source: https://aima.gov.pt/pt/viver/autorizacao-de-residencia-regime-e-requisitos-gerais-art-o-77-o-n-o-1 (Agência para a Integração, Migrações e Asilo (AIMA))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### 3. Get a social security number (NISS) - pt-niss

- Category: other
- Who: Foreign nationals who will work in Portugal, employed or self-employed
- Fee: Free
- How: Fill in the online request form on the Segurança Social portal with your passport, work visa, residence permit or permit application proof, and proof of employment. You get a registered letter at your address and then collect the number at a Segurança Social desk (book through the SIGA app or by phone on 210 548 888 or 300 088 888).
- Notes: Your employer can apply for you as your representative with a power of attorney (procuração) or form PA-12.
- Needs: passport, visa, residence-permit, employment-contract
- Source: https://www.gov.pt/guias/como-pedir-o-nif-e-o-niss-para-pessoas-estrangeiras-em-portugal (Government of Portugal (gov.pt) / Instituto da Segurança Social)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### 4. Get a national health service user number (número de utente do SNS) - pt-sns-user-number

- Category: health-insurance
- Who: Foreign residents who will use public health services
- How: Go to your local health centre (centro de saúde) or a public hospital; a user number is assigned at your first visit.
- Notes: Having a user number does not by itself mean the SNS will cover your costs. The page does not list documents or a fee.
- Source: https://www.gov.pt/servicos/pedir-o-numero-de-utente-do-sns (Administração Central do Sistema de Saúde (ACSS))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### 5. Activate the Chave Móvel Digital (digital ID login) - pt-chave-movel-digital

- Category: identity
- Who: Residents who want to sign in to Portuguese public online services
- Fee: Free
- How: If you do not hold a Portuguese Cartão de Cidadão, activate it in person at a public service counter with your passport or residence document and your own mobile phone number.
- Notes: Activating with a residence document needs a Portuguese NIF; activating with a passport does not. The key stays valid as long as the linked document. Some residence certificates cannot be used for digital signatures.
- Needs: passport, residence-permit
- Source: https://www.gov.pt/servicos/ativar-a-chave-movel-digital (Agência para a Modernização Administrativa (AMA), gov.pt)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### 6. Open a basic bank account (serviços mínimos bancários) - pt-basic-bank-account

- Category: bank-account
- Who: People who do not already hold another current account in Portugal
- Fee: Annual charges capped at 1% of the IAS, which is €5.37 in 2026
- How: Ask any bank of your choice for a minimum banking services account, or ask your bank to convert your only existing account at no cost. The bank must open it or refuse within 10 days and cannot require a minimum deposit or other products.
- Notes: Includes a debit card and 48 interbank transfers a year through online banking. The bank can close the account if you stop being a legal resident of the EU. The brochure does not list documents; banks will ask for identification.
- Source: https://clientebancario.bportugal.pt/sites/default/files/2026-06/BrochuraSMB.pdf (Banco de Portugal)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### 7. Exchange your foreign driving licence for a Portuguese one - pt-driving-licence-exchange

- Category: driving-licence
- Who: Residents holding a licence from outside the EU/EEA
- Deadline: Within 2 years of getting residence for licences from bilateral-agreement countries; immediately for licences from countries outside the international road conventions
- How: Fill in the online request on IMT's 'A Minha Carta de Condução' portal, then go to an IMT counter to give biometric data. You may have to sit a driving exam, paying the exam fee on site.
- Notes: Licences from Angola, Brazil, Cape Verde, the UK, Switzerland and other OECD and CPLP countries can be used without exchange; non-residents may drive for 185 days. The new licence arrives in about 60 days and a provisional permit covers the wait. The page does not list documents or the exchange fee.
- Needs: application-form
- Source: https://www.gov.pt/servicos/trocar-carta-de-conducao-estrangeira-por-portuguesa (Instituto da Mobilidade e dos Transportes (IMT))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### 8. Register as self-employed with the tax office (início de atividade) - pt-abrir-atividade

- Category: business-registration
- Who: Freelancers and independent workers
- Deadline: Before you start work, and no later than the day you start
- How: Submit a declaration of start of activity (declaração de início de atividade) to the tax authority.
- Notes: You need a NIF first. The page read does not give the channel, documents or fee.
- Source: https://www.gov.pt/servicos/abrir-atividade-nas-financas (Autoridade Tributária e Aduaneira (AT))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

## Local customs

- alcohol: Drinks poured in bars are often stronger than visitors expect, so pace yourself. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/portugal/safety-and-security (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (UK)), checked 07 Nov 2026
- drugs-and-medicines: Drug offences, cannabis included, carry heavy penalties, and this applies to people only passing through in transit too. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/portugal/safety-and-security (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (UK)), checked 07 Nov 2026
- other: Gambling is only legal in licensed venues. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/portugal/safety-and-security (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (UK)), checked 07 Nov 2026
- other: You can be fined for ignoring warning signs about eroding cliffs, and starting a fire is an offence even if it was an accident. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/portugal/safety-and-security (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (UK)), checked 07 Nov 2026
- public-behaviour: Carry photo ID. Police can ask for it and hold you until they confirm who you are, and ID is required when driving. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/portugal/safety-and-security (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (UK)), checked 07 Nov 2026
- public-behaviour: Albufeira has a behaviour code with on-the-spot fines from €150 to €1,800 for antisocial conduct in public. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/portugal/safety-and-security (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (UK)), checked 07 Nov 2026
