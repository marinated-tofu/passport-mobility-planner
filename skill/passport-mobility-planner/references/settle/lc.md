## Long-term residence and citizenship routes

### Saint Lucia CIP - Enterprise / infrastructure project - lc-cip-enterprise-project

- Document: citizenship
- Purpose: citizenship through business or infrastructure investment
- Fee: USD 50,000 administration fee (options 1-2); option 3 administration USD 15,000-30,000 by family size; plus processing and due diligence fees
- Minimum investment: USD 3,500,000 sole applicant; USD 6,000,000 joint venture with at least USD 1,000,000 each; or USD 250,000 (option 3, applicant with up to three dependants)
- Waiting time: About 90 days
- How to apply: Only through a licensed Authorised Agent
- For ranking: fee about USD 60,000, 90 days, investment about USD 250,000
- Required documents: application-form
- Notes: Option labels are inferred from the page layout under a National Infrastructure and Road Improvement heading.
- Source: https://www.cipsaintlucia.com/citizenship-by-investment (Citizenship by Investment Unit, Government of Saint Lucia)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial
