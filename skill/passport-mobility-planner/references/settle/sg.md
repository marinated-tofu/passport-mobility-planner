## Long-term residence and citizenship routes

### Global Investor Programme (GIP) - sg-global-investor-programme

- Document: residence-permit
- Purpose: permanent residence for business owners, founders and family office principals
- Fee: SGD 20,000 non-refundable EDB application fee; SGD 100 ICA processing fee per applicant
- Minimum investment: Option A: at least SGD 10 million in a new or expanded Singapore business. Option B: SGD 25 million in a GIP-select fund. Option C: Singapore single family office with at least SGD 200 million AUM, at least SGD 50 million deployed in Singapore-listed equities
- Waiting time: About 12 months
- Validity: Re-Entry Permit of 5 years; renewed for 5 or 3 years depending on criteria met
- Stay: For a 5-year renewal the investor or all dependants must have lived in Singapore more than half the time; a 3-year renewal accepts either this or the business condition
- How to apply: Submit the GIP e-application and Form 4 to EDB (Contact Singapore), followed by an interview; ICA then issues Approval-in-Principle
- Eligibility: Established business owner (3+ years, turnover SGD 200m+), next-generation owner (family business turnover SGD 500m+), founder of a company valued at SGD 500m+ with VC/PE backing, or family office principal (SGD 200m+ net investible assets), in a listed industry
- Proof of funds: Audited financials of the qualifying company; for Option C, bank statements showing at least SGD 200 million net investible assets
- For ranking: fee about USD 15,500, 365 days, investment about USD 7,700,000
- Required documents: passport, passport-photo, application-form, business-registration, bank-statements, cv, birth-certificate, marriage-certificate, other
- Also unlocks:
  - Panama: visa-free entry (valid (vigente) residence). Rule pa-entry-with-residence in countries/pa.md
- Notes: Approval-in-Principle lasts 6 months and the investment must be made within it. Since 21 February 2025 Option C money must go into Singapore-listed equities. Factsheet dated 5 May 2025.
- Source: https://www.edb.gov.sg/content/dam/edb-en/how-we-help/global-investor-programme/GIP%20Factsheet.pdf (Singapore Economic Development Board (Contact Singapore))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

## Steps in order

### 1. Check that your employer has bought your medical insurance (S Pass holders) - sg-s-pass-medical-insurance

- Category: health-insurance
- Who: S Pass holders; the employer must buy and maintain the cover
- Deadline: Before the S Pass can be issued or renewed
- How: Your employer buys a plan covering at least SGD 60,000 a year for inpatient care and day surgery and declares it before the pass is issued. The cost cannot be passed on to you.
- Notes: Employment Pass holders are not covered by this MOM requirement; check your employer's benefits instead.
- Source: https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/medical-insurance (Ministry of Manpower, Singapore)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### 2. Get your Employment Pass issued after you arrive - sg-get-pass-issued

- Category: residence-registration
- Who: Employment Pass holders with an in-principle approval (IPA) letter
- Deadline: Within 6 months of the in-principle approval
- Fee: SGD 225 per pass, plus SGD 30 per Multiple Journey Visa if applicable
- How: Once you are in Singapore, your employer logs in to EP eService with your passport details and Singapore address and pays the fee. You both get a notification letter by email that lets you work while you wait for the card.
- Notes: The IPA letter says whether a medical examination is needed after arrival; if so, a completed medical examination form may be required. The notification letter is valid for 1 month.
- Needs: passport, medical-certificate
- Source: https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/apply-for-a-pass (Ministry of Manpower, Singapore)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### 3. Register fingerprints and photo at EPSC and receive your pass card - sg-register-fingerprints-collect-card

- Category: residence-registration
- Who: Employment Pass holders whose notification letter says registration is required
- Deadline: Within 2 weeks after the pass is issued
- How: Your employer books an appointment at the Employment Pass Services Centre. Bring your original passport, the appointment letter, the notification letter and a phone with a Singapore number and the Singpass and SGWorkPass apps installed.
- Notes: MOM sets up your Singpass account at this visit. The card is delivered within 5 working days after registration; the holder shows a passport or digital work pass on delivery.
- Needs: passport, other
- Source: https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/apply-for-a-pass (Ministry of Manpower, Singapore)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### 4. Register for Singpass, the national digital identity - sg-singpass

- Category: identity
- Who: FIN holders aged 15 and above
- How: Go to singpass.gov.sg, choose Register, enter your details and complete face verification, then set a password and contact details.
- Notes: Check that the address on your FIN card is correct first. A mobile number is mandatory if your registered address is in Singapore, and only Singapore-registered numbers can be used. Employment Pass holders may already be registered at the EPSC visit.
- Needs: residence-permit
- Source: https://portal.singpass.gov.sg/home/ui/assets/pdf/Singpass_Registration_Guide.pdf (Singpass, Government Technology Agency of Singapore)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### 5. Check that you may rent the flat or room before you sign - sg-check-rental-rules

- Category: housing
- Who: Foreigners renting an HDB flat or bedroom, or a private home
- How: To rent an HDB flat or bedroom you need an Employment Pass, S Pass, Work Permit, Student Pass, Dependant's Pass or Long-Term Visit Pass valid for at least 6 months. Ask the owner for HDB's approval to rent out the flat and proof of ownership.
- Notes: HDB rentals must last at least 6 months, and non-Malaysian foreigners are subject to a neighbourhood and block quota (https://www.hdb.gov.sg/renting-a-flat/renting-from-open-market/regulations). Private homes must be rented for at least three consecutive months with at most six unrelated occupants (https://www.ura.gov.sg/guidelines/property-and-business-owners/property/renting-property/). Tourists cannot rent HDB flats. HDB rental data: https://www.hdb.gov.sg/renting-a-flat/renting-from-open-market/rental-statistics
- Needs: residence-permit, tenancy-contract
- Source: https://www.hdb.gov.sg/renting-a-flat/renting-from-open-market/eligibility (Housing & Development Board (HDB), Singapore)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### 6. Verify the agent and the listing to avoid rental scams - sg-avoid-rental-scams

- Category: housing
- Who: Anyone renting a home
- How: Check the agent's phone number on the CEA Public Register and view the property in person before paying anything. Pay the owner directly by a traceable method such as bank transfer, not the agent.
- Notes: Warning signs include a deposit asked before viewing, contact only by WhatsApp or an overseas number, pressure to pay quickly and third parties handling the viewing. ScamShield Helpline 1799.
- Source: https://www.cea.gov.sg/consumers/rental-scams/ (Council for Estate Agencies (CEA), Singapore)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### 7. Pay stamp duty on your tenancy agreement - sg-stamp-tenancy-agreement

- Category: housing
- Who: Parties to a lease or tenancy agreement
- Fee: 0.4% of total rent for leases of 4 years or less; exempt if average annual rent is SGD 1,000 or less
- How: Stamp the lease through IRAS's Stamp Lease/Tenancy Documents digital service; the Stamp Certificate is the proof the agreement is duly stamped.
- Notes: Duty is based on the contractual or market rent, whichever is higher. The page read does not state who pays or the payment deadline; see IRAS 'Who Should Pay Stamp Duty' and 'When to Pay Stamp Duty'.
- Needs: tenancy-contract
- Source: https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/renting-a-property (Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### 8. Update MOM when your residential address changes - sg-update-address

- Category: address-registration
- Who: Employment Pass holders or their employers
- Deadline: Within 5 days of the change
- How: Log in to EP eService and update the address; it takes effect immediately. Print the acknowledgement page and keep it with your pass card.
- Notes: The new address must meet MOM's housing requirements. Late updates can lead to a penalty.
- Source: https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/notify-mom-of-changes (Ministry of Manpower, Singapore)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### 9. Check on myTax Portal whether you must file an income tax return - sg-income-tax-filing

- Category: tax-number
- Who: Individuals earning income in Singapore
- Deadline: File between 1 March and 18 April each year if required
- How: Log in to myTax Portal with Singpass (or a Singpass Foreign user Account) to see if you are required to file. Non-residents who derived income from Singapore in the preceding year must file.
- Notes: There is no separate tax registration step on this page; the page does not say which number IRAS uses as your tax reference. Residents must also file if income exceeded SGD 22,000 in the preceding year.
- Source: https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/employees/basic-guide-for-new-individual-taxpayers (Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### 10. Convert your foreign driving licence after passing the Basic Theory Test - sg-convert-driving-licence

- Category: driving-licence
- Who: Foreigners residing in Singapore for more than 12 months
- How: Pass the Basic Theory Test in Singapore, then apply to convert your valid foreign licence. Foreigners staying under 12 months can drive on their foreign licence with an International Driving Permit or official English translation.
- Notes: Bring a recent colour passport photo, your work pass and passport, the valid foreign licence (with an extract of records if the first issue date is not shown, and an official translation if not in English), and proof of at least one year's residency in the issuing country. Work Permit and S Pass holders get a Class 3C licence. Fees are not listed on this page.
- Needs: passport-photo, passport, residence-permit, other
- Source: https://www.police.gov.sg/Knowledge-Hub/Traffic/Traffic-Matters/Singapore-Driving-Licence (Singapore Police Force (Traffic Police))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### 11. Register a sole proprietorship or partnership with ACRA - sg-register-business

- Category: business-registration
- Who: Foreigners starting a sole business or partnership
- Fee: SGD 100 for one year (SGD 160 for three years if CPF Board conditions are met)
- How: Foreigners with Singpass can register on Bizfile themselves if they reserved the business name; those without Singpass must engage a corporate service provider to register for them.
- Notes: Each owner needs an NRIC or FIN. If all owners live overseas, an authorised representative ordinarily resident in Singapore is required. Business and contact addresses cannot be P.O. Boxes.
- Needs: residence-permit
- Source: https://www.acra.gov.sg/register/business/registering-different-business-structures/sole-proprietorship-or-partnership/ (Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

## Local customs

- alcohol: Drinking in public places outside licensed venues is banned from 10.30pm to 7am, and Geylang and Little India have extra weekend and holiday drinking bans. Drunken or disorderly behaviour is prosecuted. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/singapore/safety-and-security (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, UK), checked 07 Nov 2026
- drugs-and-medicines: Drug penalties are severe, from prison and caning up to the death penalty for trafficking, and traces of drugs in your body on arrival can lead to arrest. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/singapore/safety-and-security (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, UK), checked 07 Nov 2026
- drugs-and-medicines: You may bring up to three months' supply of your own medicine with a prescription or doctor's letter, in the original labelled container. Medicines with controlled substances need HSA approval applied for at least two weeks before arrival. Source: https://www.hsa.gov.sg/travelling-with-medication-and-medical-devices/personal-medications/ (Health Sciences Authority (HSA), Singapore), checked 07 Nov 2026
- lgbt: Same-sex activity is legal, but same-sex marriages and partnerships are not recognised, so a same-sex partner cannot easily get a Dependant's Pass. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/singapore/safety-and-security (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, UK), checked 07 Nov 2026
- other: Vapes and e-cigarettes are banned, including bringing them in for personal use. You must be 21 or over to buy or use tobacco, and smoking is limited to designated areas. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/singapore/safety-and-security (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, UK), checked 07 Nov 2026
- other: Chewing gum for sale, chewed or injected tobacco, replica guns and some radio equipment are prohibited imports; declare anything that may be taxable or restricted. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/singapore/entry-requirements (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, UK), checked 07 Nov 2026
- public-behaviour: Littering brings on-the-spot fines and vandalism can mean caning. Foreigners who are not permanent residents may not join outdoor demonstrations or give public talks on political, racial or religious topics without a permit. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/singapore/safety-and-security (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, UK), checked 07 Nov 2026
- religion: Jehovah's Witness meetings and publications are illegal, and similar measures apply to the Unification Church and Shincheonji. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/singapore/safety-and-security (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, UK), checked 07 Nov 2026
