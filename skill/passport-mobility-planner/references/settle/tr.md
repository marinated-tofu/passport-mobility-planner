## Long-term residence and citizenship routes

### Turkish citizenship by investment (deposit, bonds, fund shares or pension) - tr-citizenship-bank-deposit

- Document: citizenship
- Purpose: citizenship through financial investment
- Minimum investment: USD 500,000 deposited in Turkish banks, or in government bonds, real estate or venture capital fund shares, or private pension, each held at least 3 years
- How to apply: Certified by BDDK (deposits), Ministry of Treasury and Finance (bonds), Capital Markets Board (funds) or SEDDK (pension)
- Eligibility: Granted at the President's discretion
- For ranking: fee unknown, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 500,000
- Required documents: passport, bank-statements, other
- Notes: Amounts follow the 2018 citizenship regulation as amended.
- Source: https://www.invest.gov.tr/en/investmentguide/pages/acquiring-property-and-citizenship.aspx (Presidency of the Republic of Turkiye Investment Office)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### Turkish citizenship by investment (fixed capital or job creation) - tr-citizenship-fixed-capital-or-employment

- Document: citizenship
- Purpose: citizenship through business investment
- Minimum investment: USD 500,000 fixed capital investment, or creating jobs for at least 50 people
- How to apply: Certified by the Ministry of Industry and Technology (capital) or the Ministry of Labor and Social Security (employment)
- Eligibility: Granted at the President's discretion
- For ranking: fee unknown, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 500,000
- Required documents: passport, business-registration, other
- Notes: No holding period stated for these two routes.
- Source: https://www.invest.gov.tr/en/investmentguide/pages/acquiring-property-and-citizenship.aspx (Presidency of the Republic of Turkiye Investment Office)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### Turkish citizenship by investment (real estate) - tr-citizenship-real-estate

- Document: citizenship
- Purpose: citizenship through property investment
- Minimum investment: USD 400,000 in real estate
- How to apply: Register the purchase at the land registry (TKGM) stating it is for citizenship, obtain a certificate of eligibility from the Ministry of Environment, Urbanization and Climate Change, then apply
- Eligibility: Granted at the President's discretion
- For ranking: fee unknown, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 400,000
- Required documents: passport, passport-photo, other
- Notes: The title deed carries a 3-year no-sale annotation. A municipal valuation and earthquake insurance are needed.
- Source: https://www.invest.gov.tr/en/investmentguide/pages/acquiring-property-and-citizenship.aspx (Presidency of the Republic of Turkiye Investment Office)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified
