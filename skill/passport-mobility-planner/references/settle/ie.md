### Immigrant Investor Programme (IIP) - closed - ie-immigrant-investor-programme

- Document: residence-permit
- Purpose: closed investor residence route
- How to apply: Closed to new applications from 15 February 2023
- For ranking: fee unknown, waiting days unknown
- Also unlocks:
  - Georgia: visa-free entry (valid on the day of entry into Georgia). Rule ge-entry-with-residence in countries/ge.md
  - South Korea: visa-free transit (permanent resident card of one of the 32 listed European countries; travelling via Korea to or from it). Not for nationals of SY, SD, IR, MK, CU, XK, PS, AF, IQ, NG, GH, YE, GM, SN, BD, KG, PK, SO, UZ, NP, CM, LK, MM, EG. Rule kr-transit-with-european-residence in countries/kr.md

- Notes: Closed to new applications on 15 February 2023. Applications already on hand are processed during the wind-down; approved investors are unaffected. Historically EUR 500,000 to EUR 2 million.
