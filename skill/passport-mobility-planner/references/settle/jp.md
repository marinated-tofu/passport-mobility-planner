## Long-term residence and citizenship routes

### Business Manager status of residence - jp-business-manager

- Document: residence-permit
- Purpose: residence to run or manage a business in Japan
- Minimum investment: JPY 30 million or more in capital (paid-in capital for a corporation, or total invested funds for a sole proprietor)
- Validity: 5 years, 3 years, 1 year, 6 months, 4 months or 3 months
- How to apply: Apply for a Certificate of Eligibility at a regional Immigration Services Bureau, then obtain the visa at a Japanese mission abroad
- Eligibility: At least one full-time employee who is Japanese or holds a qualifying status; B2-level Japanese (e.g. JLPT N2) held by the applicant or that employee; 3+ years of management experience or a relevant master's, doctoral or professional degree; a home office is generally not accepted
- Proof of funds: Evidence of the JPY 30 million capital, such as a balance sheet or proof of invested funds
- For ranking: fee unknown, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 200,000
- Required documents: passport, passport-photo, application-form, business-registration, tenancy-contract, employment-contract, degree-certificate, other
- Also unlocks:
  - Georgia: visa-free entry (valid on the day of entry into Georgia). Rule ge-entry-with-residence in countries/ge.md
  - Mexico: visa-free entry (permanent residence card, valid at entry; any nationality). Rule mx-entry-with-residence in countries/mx.md
  - Panama: visa-free entry (valid (vigente) residence). Rule pa-entry-with-residence in countries/pa.md
  - Singapore: visa-free transit (long-term pass valid at least 1 month from entry into Singapore). Only for nationals of IN. Rule sg-vftf-with-residence-in in countries/sg.md
  - Taiwan: electronic travel authorisation (valid resident or permanent resident certificate, or one that expired less than 10 years before arrival). Only for nationals of IN, ID, LA, MM, VN. Rule tw-tac-with-residence in countries/tw.md
- Notes: Stricter criteria apply from 16 October 2025, including a business plan checked by an outside SME consultant, CPA or tax accountant. Existing holders renewing before 16 October 2028 are not refused only for missing the new rules.
- Source: https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/resources/10_00237.html (Immigration Services Agency of Japan (Ministry of Justice))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

## Steps in order

### 1. Receive your residence card on arrival - jp-residence-card

- Category: residence-registration
- Who: Mid- to long-term residents (not short-term visitors, diplomats or people staying 3 months or less)
- How: The residence card is issued when landing permission is granted. If no card is issued at the port of entry, your passport is marked that a card will be issued later and it is sent after you register your address.
- Notes: JETRO says a Specified Residence Card combining the residence card and My Number Card was scheduled from June 2026; check which card you receive. A lost or damaged card must be re-issued within 14 days at the Regional Immigration Services Bureau.
- Needs: passport, visa
- Source: https://www.jetro.go.jp/en/invest/setting_up/section2/page7/ (Japan External Trade Organization (JETRO))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### 2. Rent a home - jp-rent-housing

- Category: housing
- Who: Foreign residents renting private housing
- How: Apply through a real estate agent with your passport, residence card, proof of earnings and an employment or school certificate, and use a guarantor or a rental guarantee company. Before signing, the agent must explain the important points of the contract, including the deposit, key money and renewal fee.
- Notes: MLIT says upfront payments (deposit, key money, agency fee, insurance, first month's rent) are roughly 4 to 7 months' rent, and a guarantee company often charges about half a month's rent for two years. On moving out the deposit is returned minus repairs for damage you caused; ageing and normal wear and tear are the landlord's cost (MLIT restoration leaflet, https://www.mlit.go.jp/jutakukentiku/house/content/001595135.pdf). Local governments and international exchange associations offer housing consultation for foreigners.
- Needs: passport, residence-permit, proof-of-income, employment-letter, tenancy-contract
- Source: https://www.mlit.go.jp/common/001317844.pdf (Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT), Japan)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### 3. Register your address at the municipal office - jp-resident-registration

- Category: address-registration
- Who: Mid- to long-term residents with a residence card
- Deadline: Within 14 days of establishing a place of residence
- How: Go to the counter in charge at the city, ward or town office where you live and bring your residence card. If no card was issued at entry, bring your passport marked that the card will be issued later.
- Notes: This also enters you in the Basic Resident Register and creates your residence certificate (juminhyo). Moving again later needs a new notification within 14 days of moving.
- Needs: residence-permit, passport
- Source: https://www.jetro.go.jp/en/invest/setting_up/section2/page7/ (Japan External Trade Organization (JETRO))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### 4. Receive your My Number and apply for the My Number Card - jp-my-number

- Category: identity
- Who: Residents registered in the Basic Resident Register
- How: Your Individual Number Notice and an application form are mailed to your registered address. Apply for the card online, by smartphone QR code, by mail or at some photo booths, then collect it at the municipal counter by the date on the issuance notice.
- Notes: JETRO says the notice arrives about two to three weeks after your first residence certificate is created. J-LIS says production takes about 3 weeks and mentions a 1,000 yen issuance fee without saying whether it applies to first issuance, so fee is left empty. The card is optional.
- Needs: passport-photo, application-form, residence-permit
- Source: https://www.kojinbango-card.go.jp/en/ (Japan Agency for Local Authority Information Systems (J-LIS))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### 5. Join public health insurance - jp-health-insurance

- Category: health-insurance
- Who: Residents staying more than 3 months; employees join through their employer, everyone else joins National Health Insurance
- Deadline: Within 14 days of becoming eligible
- How: If you are not covered by employee health insurance through your workplace, sign up for National Health Insurance at your ward office. Bring photo ID such as your residence card or passport.
- Notes: Rule and deadline from Yokohama City; other municipalities run the same national scheme but counters and documents may differ. Premiums depend on income and municipality.
- Needs: residence-permit, other
- Source: https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/lang/residents/en/insurance/kokuhoenglish.html (City of Yokohama)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### 6. Open a bank account - jp-bank-account

- Category: bank-account
- Who: Foreign residents with a residence card
- How: Bring your residence card, passport, a hanko seal (some banks accept a signature) and an employee or student ID card. Without such a card, go with someone from your company or school.
- Notes: The FSA pamphlet says required items differ between institutions and that banks verify name, address, period of stay, status of residence and employment. Tell the bank when your address, period of stay or status changes.
- Needs: residence-permit, passport, other
- Source: https://www.fsa.go.jp/en/user/03.english_2024.pdf (Financial Services Agency, Japan)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### 7. Convert your foreign driving licence (gaimen kirikae) - jp-driving-licence

- Category: driving-licence
- Who: Holders of a valid foreign licence who are registered residents of Japan
- How: Apply at the driver's licence centre of your prefecture with your foreign licence, its Japanese translation and a copy of your residence record (juminhyo). Since October 2025 the knowledge check has 50 questions with a 90 percent pass mark and the driving check is stricter.
- Notes: From 1 October 2025 short-term visitors cannot convert a licence and a residence record copy is required regardless of nationality (NPA). Prefectural police pages state an extra rule that you must have stayed at least 3 months in the issuing country after getting the licence. Fees vary by prefecture and were not on the page read.
- Needs: residence-permit, passport, other
- Source: https://www.cas.go.jp/jp/seisaku/symbiotic_society/pdf/Initiatives_egarding_foreign_license_conversion.pdf (National Police Agency (published by the Cabinet Secretariat))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### 8. Register a company at the Legal Affairs Bureau - jp-business-registration

- Category: business-registration
- Who: Foreign nationals or companies setting up a kabushiki kaisha or other company in Japan
- How: Check the name, have the articles of incorporation certified by a Japanese notary, pay in the capital, then apply to the Legal Affairs Bureau for registration and register the company seal.
- Notes: Representative directors no longer need to live in Japan (Ministry of Justice). Foreign nationals may sign instead of sealing and submit a signature certificate from their home country or a notary; foreign-language documents need a Japanese translation. JETRO puts the whole process at about two to three months. Registration and notary fees were not on the pages read.
- Needs: passport, business-registration, other
- Source: https://www.jetro.go.jp/en/invest/setting_up/section1/page3/ (Japan External Trade Organization (JETRO))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

## Local customs

- alcohol: Drinking in public is generally allowed, but some areas ban it on certain days such as New Year's Eve. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/japan/safety-and-security (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, UK), checked 07 Nov 2026
- drugs-and-medicines: Japan has zero tolerance for illegal drugs, with long prison terms for trafficking, and even small amounts received by post have led to arrests. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/japan/safety-and-security (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, UK), checked 07 Nov 2026
- drugs-and-medicines: Some common medicines are banned under the anti-stimulant law, including Vicks inhalers, cold and allergy remedies with pseudoephedrine and some codeine painkillers. Check with a Japanese embassy before bringing medication. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/japan/health (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, UK), checked 07 Nov 2026
- lgbt: Same-sex relationships are legal but there is no national anti-discrimination protection and same-sex marriage is not recognised, though some local authorities issue partnership certificates. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/japan/safety-and-security (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, UK), checked 07 Nov 2026
- other: You must carry your passport or residence card at all times, and police can arrest you if you cannot show proof of your status. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/japan/safety-and-security (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, UK), checked 07 Nov 2026
- other: Smoking on the street is illegal in Tokyo and some other cities, so smoke only in marked smoking areas. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/japan/safety-and-security (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, UK), checked 07 Nov 2026
- other: Because tattoos are linked with organised crime, some hot springs, pools, beaches and gyms refuse entry to tattooed people or ask them to cover up. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/japan/safety-and-security (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, UK), checked 07 Nov 2026
- public-behaviour: Loud behaviour draws disapproval and public displays of affection are uncommon. Police can hold suspects for up to 23 days without charge, even for minor offences. Source: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/japan/safety-and-security (Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, UK), checked 07 Nov 2026
