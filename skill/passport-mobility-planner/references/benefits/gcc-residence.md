## Armenia

### Visa-free entry - am-temporary-visa-exemption-gcc-residence

- Stay: up to 180 days within one year
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by Qatar, United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman; Valid for at least 6 months from entry; shown as a physical card or passport sticker with title, name, nationality, birth date and validity in Latin script and Gregorian dates
- Notes: Temporary exemption from 1 July 2026 to 1 July 2027. It covers nationals of 111 countries listed in a PDF linked from the page, which was not opened, so check that list for your nationality. US, EU and Schengen residence permits also qualify.
- Source: https://www.mfa.am/en/visa/ (Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Armenia)

## Georgia

### Visa-free entry - ge-visa-free-gcc-visa-or-residence

- Stay: 90 days in any 180-day period
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by Qatar, United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman; valid on the day of entry, shown by a travel or other document
- Notes: All six GCC states are on Georgia's list of countries whose visa or residence permit lets a foreigner enter without a visa (a visa from these states also counts). Since the April 2025 amendment, citizens of 17 countries (Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Nigeria and others) face a stricter test; the Georgian embassy in the UAE confirms it for UAE permits (see the next rule). Check that rule before relying on this one.
- Source: https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/2867377?publication=1 (Legislative Herald of Georgia (Government Ordinance No. 256 of 5 June 2015, as amended))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### Visa-free entry - ge-visa-free-uae-residence-restricted-nationalities

- Stay: 90 days in any 180-day period
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by United Arab Emirates; Multiple-entry UAE visa or UAE residence permit valid for at least 1 year on the day of entry to Georgia
- Only for nationals of: Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Cameroon, Congo, Congo, The Democratic Republic of the, Côte d'Ivoire, Ethiopia, Eritrea, Ghana, Morocco, Nigeria, Pakistan, Somalia, Sudan, Syria, Tanzania, Uganda, Yemen
- Notes: Applies to citizens of 17 listed countries. The page says only "Congo", so both Congo states are listed here. If their UAE residence has less than a year left, they need a C1 tourist visa through the Georgian e-visa system. For other nationalities, a UAE permit must be valid on entry and departure and the passport valid at least 90 days.
- Source: https://uae.mfa.gov.ge/visa-information (Embassy of Georgia in the UAE, Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Georgia)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

## Oman

### E-visa - om-evisa-29a-gcc-resident

- Purpose: tourism
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by Qatar, United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain; main traveller must hold an approved profession
- Notes: The ROP eVisa system has a GCC Resident Tourist Visa (type 29A) for the main traveller and a companion visa (type 29B). The FAQ does not state stay length, fee or the profession list; the eligibility wizard shows these after you enter your details.
- Source: https://evisa.rop.gov.om/Evisa-home-theme/faq_pages/faq_en.html (Royal Oman Police eVisa portal, Sultanate of Oman)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### Visa on arrival - om-visa-gcc-residents-on-arrival

- Stay: 28 days from entry; renewable once for one week
- Purpose: tourism
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by Qatar, United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain; Valid GCC residence; profession approved by the Directorate General of Passports and Residence; arriving from the country of residence directly or via another GCC state; passport valid at least 6 months
- Notes: Single entry. Issued at any Omani entry point or at Omani missions in GCC states, with proof of profession. Companions and relatives travelling with the resident can get it too. Fee OMR 5. The page is in Arabic only.
- Source: https://www.rop.gov.om/arabic/Visas.aspx?VisaName=AgccVisa (Royal Oman Police, Sultanate of Oman)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

## Qatar

### E-visa - qa-hayya-a2-gcc-resident-visa

- Stay: up to 30 days from entry; single entry
- Purpose: visit
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman; GCC residence valid for 3 months from the application date, and the GCC ID valid at least 3 months from arrival; passport valid at least 3 months from arrival
- Notes: Apply on the Hayya portal (A2 visa), fee QAR 100. You should travel from your country of residence and bring a return ticket if flying. The job title you pick must match the one on your residence permit; any profession can be entered. Each GCC resident needs their own application; family members without GCC residence apply for an A1 or A3 visa.
- Source: https://hayya.qa/en/faq?tab=a2 (Hayya portal, State of Qatar)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### Visa on arrival - qa-visa-on-arrival-gcc-residents

- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman; valid GCC residence permit
- Notes: Hayya says visas on arrival are still available to GCC residents, as an alternative to the A2 e-visa. The page gives no stay length, profession or validity rules for the on-arrival route.
- Source: https://hayya.qa/en/faq?tab=a2 (Hayya portal, State of Qatar)

## Saudi Arabia

### E-visa - sa-tourist-evisa-gcc-residents

- Stay: up to 90 days; multiple-entry visa valid for one year
- Purpose: tourism
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by Qatar, United Arab Emirates, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman; GCC residence visa valid for at least 3 months, in a profession published on visitsaudi.com (per the Ministry of Tourism regulation); passport valid at least 6 months
- Notes: Visit Saudi confirms GCC residents can get the tourist visa as an eVisa or via an embassy, and that a resident can add spouse, parents and children to the account after applying. Medical insurance is bought during the application. The Ministry of Tourism regulation adds the 3-month residence and approved-profession conditions and a SAR 300 fee; the Visit Saudi page does not show the profession list, so whether all professions now qualify is unclear. No Hajj on this visa and no work.
- Source: https://www.visitsaudi.com/en/plan-your-trip/visa-regulations (Saudi Tourism Authority (Visit Saudi); regulation by Ministry of Tourism, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

## United Arab Emirates

### E-visa - ae-evisa-gcc-residents

- Stay: 30 days from entry; extendable once for 30 days (entry permit valid 30 days from issue)
- Purpose: visit (listed under tourist visas)
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman; GCC residence valid for at least 1 year from the arrival date; passport valid at least 6 months from arrival; profession must be on the approved list (assessed by the authority)
- Notes: Must be issued online before travel, through ICP or GDRFA Dubai. Companions of the GCC resident can be included. The profession check is case by case, and the page does not publish the list.
- Source: https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/Types-of-visas/tourist-visa (The Official Platform of the UAE Government (u.ae))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified
