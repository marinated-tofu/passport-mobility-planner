## Ethiopia

### Stopover program - et-ethiopian-addis-ababa-stopover

- Stay: connection of 8 to 24 hours (usually 1 night)
- Purpose: free hotel, meals and transfers during a long connection in Addis Ababa
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - ticket; single Ethiopian Airlines ticket with both flights operated by Ethiopian, connecting in Addis Ababa for 8 to 24 hours
- Notes: Free hotel (usually one night), meals and airport transfers. The voucher is issued automatically at the origin station or at the transit desk in Addis Ababa. The airline helps arrange the transit visa where needed. A longer visa-free stopover of 24 hours to 7 days was announced but is not yet in force, so it is not included. Page reviewed August 2026.
- Source: https://www.ethiopianairlines.com/services/services-at-the-airport/addis-ababa-stopovers (Ethiopian Airlines)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

## Saudi Arabia

### Stopover program - sa-saudi-stopover-transit-visa

- Stay: up to 96 hours; visa valid 3 months, single entry
- Purpose: stopover in Saudi Arabia, including tourism and Umrah
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - ticket; flight booked with Saudia or flynas through Saudi Arabia; the stopover visa is requested during the booking
- Notes: The visa itself has no fee, but there is an application charge of about USD 10.50 plus required medical insurance from about USD 3.45. It is issued through the airline booking, valid 3 months for one entry, with a stay of up to 96 hours. Umrah needs a Nusuk appointment. The page does not say whether the onward flight must go to a third country. A free hotel night reported elsewhere is not on this page and is not included. Page updated 16 Sep 2026.
- Source: https://www.visitsaudi.com/en/campaigns/saudi-stopover (Saudi Tourism Authority)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

## Singapore

### Stopover program - sg-changi-free-singapore-tour

- Stay: layover of 5.5 to 24 hours; tour about 2.5 hours
- Purpose: free guided city tour during a transit at Changi
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - ticket; transit or transfer at Changi Airport with a layover of 5.5 to 24 hours, bags checked through to the final destination
  - passport; able to enter Singapore; nationalities that need a visa (for example India or Pakistan) must already hold one
- Notes: Free guided tours of about 2.5 hours with four itineraries and set registration times. Passengers clear immigration once in and once out. Changi also sells paid transit tours for layovers of 6 hours or more.

- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

## Türkiye

### Stopover program - tr-turkish-airlines-stopover-hotel

- Stay: layover of at least 20 hours, up to 7 days
- Purpose: free hotel nights in Istanbul during a connection
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - ticket; Turkish Airlines international-to-international itinerary via Istanbul Airport starting in one of the eligible countries, with a layover of 20 hours or more; request at least 72 hours before the flight
- Notes: Free hotel with breakfast. Economy passengers get 1 night in a 4-star hotel; Business passengers get 2 nights in a 5-star or boutique hotel. Request it through the airline's booking channel or the country email listed on the page, at least 72 hours ahead. Only trips that start in the countries the page lists qualify, and it can be used once per journey (outbound or return). Visa costs are the passenger's. Subject to hotel availability.
- Source: https://www.turkishairlines.com/en-int/flights/stopover/ (Turkish Airlines)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### Stopover program - tr-turkish-airlines-touristanbul

- Stay: 6 to 24 hours layover
- Purpose: free guided city tour during a connection at Istanbul Airport
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - ticket; Turkish Airlines ticket (number starting 235) with an international-to-international connection at Istanbul Airport, both flights on the same booking (PNR), layover between 6 and 24 hours
- Notes: Free. Turkish Airlines runs four tour routes through Istanbul with set departure times. Book online with the PNR or sign up at the Hotel Desk on the Arrivals floor at least 30 minutes before the tour (allow about 45 minutes with passport control). The tour must end at least 1 hour 25 minutes before the next flight. Passengers arrange their own Turkish visa if needed. It cannot be used in the same layover as the Stopover hotel offer. Schedule updated 1 May 2026.
- Source: https://www.turkishairlines.com/en-int/flights/fly-different/touristanbul/ (Turkish Airlines)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

## United Arab Emirates

### Stopover program - ae-emirates-dubai-connect

- Stay: connection of 6 to 26 hours
- Purpose: free hotel, meals and transfers during a long connection in Dubai
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - ticket; single Emirates ticket (176 stock), both flights operated by Emirates (flydubai from Terminal 3 also counts) on the shortest available connection of 6 to 26 hours; minimum fare (base plus YQ) USD 450 one-way or USD 700 return, or USD 500 / USD 1000 for EK-FZ combinations
  - passport; valid for at least 6 months, and able to get UAE entry (some nationalities are refused)
- Age: adults 18+; up to 2 children when travelling with an adult
- Notes: Free in all cabins: hotel room, meals and airport transfers in Dubai. Hotels include Copthorne, Le Meridien Airport and JW Marriott Marquis or similar. Passengers must clear UAE immigration themselves. Only applies when no shorter connection was available. Subject to availability and the full terms on the page.
