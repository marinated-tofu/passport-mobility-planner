## Albania

### Visa-free entry - al-entry-with-residence

- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by any Schengen state, United States, United Kingdom, any EU state; valid residence permit, not under renewal
- Not for nationals of: Iran
- Notes: A valid residence permit from a Schengen state, an EU country, the US or the UK also waives the Albanian visa. A permit that is in the middle of renewal does not count; those travellers need an e-visa. Same 3-day departure deadline around the permit's expiry and the same exclusion of Iranian citizens. No maximum stay is stated.
- Source: https://ambasadat.gov.al/united-kingdom/en/newsroom/pyetje-te-shpeshta/ (Embassy of the Republic of Albania in the United Kingdom, Ministry for Europe and Foreign Affairs)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### Visa-free entry - al-entry-with-visa

- Unlocked by, all of:
  - visa issued by any Schengen state, United States, United Kingdom, any EU state; valid multiple-entry visa (electronic formats accepted for EU/US/UK) that has already been used to enter the issuing country or the Schengen area
- Not for nationals of: Iran
- Notes: A used, valid multiple-entry visa from a Schengen state, an EU country, the US or the UK replaces the Albanian visa. The holder must leave Albania within 3 days of the visa's expiry date (the embassy's wording on the exact cut-off is ambiguous). Iranian citizens need an Albanian visa regardless. The embassy FAQ gives no maximum stay for this route.
- Source: https://ambasadat.gov.al/united-kingdom/en/newsroom/pyetje-te-shpeshta/ (Embassy of the Republic of Albania in the United Kingdom, Ministry for Europe and Foreign Affairs)

## Mexico

### Visa-free entry - mx-entry-with-residence

- Purpose: tourism, business, transit
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by Canada, United States, Japan, United Kingdom, any Schengen state; permanent residence card, valid at entry; any nationality
- Notes: Permanent residents of Canada, the US, Japan, the UK or a Schengen country do not need a Mexican visa, whatever their nationality. Carry the residence card with the passport and fill in the FMMd. Visitors cannot switch to temporary or permanent residence inside Mexico except on humanitarian or family-unity grounds. No stay limit is given for this group on the consulate page.
- Source: https://consulmex.sre.gob.mx/montreal/index.php/es/enterate/7-servicios-consulares/servicios-a-extranjeros/159-turistasnuevoesp (Consulate General of Mexico in Montreal, Secretariat of Foreign Affairs (SRE), Government of Mexico)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### Visa-free entry - mx-entry-with-us-visa

- Purpose: tourism, business, transit
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - visa issued by United States; US visa must be valid (unexpired) at entry; any nationality
- Notes: Travellers of any nationality who would normally need a Mexican visa can skip it if they carry a current US visa. A completed digital multiple migration form (FMMd) is still required, the passport must stay valid for the whole visit, and officers may ask for proof of the trip's purpose. The consulate page gives no stay limit for this group. Other Mexican consulates describe the rule differently (some also accept Canadian, Japanese, UK or Schengen visas, some require a multiple-entry visa that was already used), so confirm with the consulate that covers you.
- Source: https://consulmex.sre.gob.mx/montreal/index.php/es/enterate/7-servicios-consulares/servicios-a-extranjeros/159-turistasnuevoesp (Consulate General of Mexico in Montreal, Secretariat of Foreign Affairs (SRE), Government of Mexico)
