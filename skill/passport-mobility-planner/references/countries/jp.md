## Visa-free transit

### Visa-free transit - jp-shore-pass-port-of-call

- Stay: under 72 hours
- Purpose: short landing at the port of call while in transit (no paid activity)
- Applies only if every check is yes. Write each check and its answer in your reply; one no means this rule does not apply.
  1. Does the traveller hold a passport? It must meet: valid passport for the onward destination; foreign passenger (not crew). Its expiry must be after the last day of the stay; write the expiry date.
  2. Does the traveller hold a ticket? It must meet: ticket for the trip out of Japan (or a carrier's letter of guarantee), leaving from the same port.
- Partial rule: if it applies, tell the traveller to confirm it on the source below.
- Notes (if the rule applies, copy every form to fill and every caveat into the reply): Landing permission at a port of call (often called a shore pass). Granted by an immigration inspector on written application; movement is generally limited to the city or town of the arrival port and paid work is banned. It is a discretionary permission, not an automatic right. Based on the Enforcement Regulation of the Immigration Control Act (official English translation); the day-to-day process at airports is not described there.
- Source: https://www.japaneselawtranslation.go.jp/en/laws/view/4180/en (Ministry of Justice, Japan (Japanese Law Translation database))

### Visa-free transit - jp-transit-pass-landing-in-transit

- Stay: up to 3 days
- Purpose: crossing Japan to board a departing flight or ship at another port (no paid activity)
- Applies only if every check is yes. Write each check and its answer in your reply; one no means this rule does not apply.
  1. Does the traveller hold a passport? It must meet: valid passport for the onward destination; foreign passenger (not crew). Its expiry must be after the last day of the stay; write the expiry date.
  2. Does the traveller hold a ticket? It must meet: ticket for the trip out of Japan (or a carrier's letter of guarantee) from a different port than the arrival port.
- Partial rule: if it applies, tell the traveller to confirm it on the source below.
- Notes (if the rule applies, copy every form to fill and every caveat into the reply): Landing permission in transit (often called a transit pass). Lets a passenger travel overland to another Japanese port to leave by plane or ship, up to 3 days, along a set route. A separate 15-day version exists for ship passengers rejoining their own vessel at another port. Discretionary; granted by an immigration inspector on written application.
- Source: https://www.japaneselawtranslation.go.jp/en/laws/view/4180/en (Ministry of Justice, Japan (Japanese Law Translation database))

## Long-term residence and citizenship routes

### Business Manager status of residence - jp-business-manager

- Document: residence-permit
- Purpose: residence to run or manage a business in Japan
- Minimum investment: JPY 30 million or more in capital (paid-in capital for a corporation, or total invested funds for a sole proprietor)
- Validity: 5 years, 3 years, 1 year, 6 months, 4 months or 3 months
- How to apply: Apply for a Certificate of Eligibility at a regional Immigration Services Bureau, then obtain the visa at a Japanese mission abroad
- Eligibility: At least one full-time employee who is Japanese or holds a qualifying status; B2-level Japanese (e.g. JLPT N2) held by the applicant or that employee; 3+ years of management experience or a relevant master's, doctoral or professional degree; a home office is generally not accepted
- Proof of funds: Evidence of the JPY 30 million capital, such as a balance sheet or proof of invested funds
- For ranking: fee unknown, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 200,000
- Required documents: passport, passport-photo, application-form, business-registration, tenancy-contract, employment-contract, degree-certificate, other
- Also unlocks:
  - Georgia: visa-free entry (valid on the day of entry into Georgia). Rule ge-entry-with-residence in countries/ge.md
  - Mexico: visa-free entry (permanent residence card, valid at entry; any nationality). Rule mx-entry-with-residence in countries/mx.md
  - Panama: visa-free entry (valid (vigente) residence). Rule pa-entry-with-residence in countries/pa.md
  - Singapore: visa-free transit (long-term pass valid at least 1 month from entry into Singapore). Only for nationals of IN. Rule sg-vftf-with-residence-in in countries/sg.md
  - Taiwan: electronic travel authorisation (valid resident or permanent resident certificate, or one that expired less than 10 years before arrival). Only for nationals of IN, ID, LA, MM, VN. Rule tw-tac-with-residence in countries/tw.md
- Notes: Stricter criteria apply from 16 October 2025, including a business plan checked by an outside SME consultant, CPA or tax accountant. Existing holders renewing before 16 October 2028 are not refused only for missing the new rules.
- Source: https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/resources/10_00237.html (Immigration Services Agency of Japan (Ministry of Justice))

## Visas and travel authorisations you can apply for

### Designated Activities visa for digital nomads - jp-digital-nomad-designated-activities

- Document: visa
- Purpose: remote work for foreign employers or clients while staying in Japan
- Stay: 6 months; cannot be extended
- How to apply: At a Japanese embassy or consulate; a Certificate of Eligibility is optional
- Eligibility: Nationals of the countries listed by the Immigration Services Agency who work remotely
- Proof of funds: Annual income of JPY 10 million or more
- For ranking: fee unknown, waiting days unknown
- Required documents: application-form, passport-photo, passport, tax-return, proof-of-income, employment-contract, health-insurance, other
- Also unlocks:
  - Georgia: visa-free entry (valid on the day of entry into Georgia). Rule ge-entry-with-visa in countries/ge.md
  - Panama: visa-free entry (valid (vigente) visa). Rule pa-entry-with-visa in countries/pa.md
  - Singapore: visa-free transit (valid at least 1 month from entry into Singapore; issued by, or valid for entry into, one of these countries). Only for nationals of IN. Rule sg-vftf-with-visa-in in countries/sg.md
  - Singapore: visa-free transit (valid visa or long-term pass for one of these countries, valid at least 1 month from the date of entry into Singapore; a used Single Journey Visa counts on the direct return trip home). Only for nationals of IN. Rule sg-vftf-india in countries/sg.md
  - South Korea: visa-free transit (Japanese group visa). Only for nationals of CN. Rule kr-chinese-group-with-japan-group-visa in countries/kr.md
  - Taiwan: electronic travel authorisation (valid entry visa (electronic visas accepted), or one that expired less than 10 years before arrival; work permits do not count). Only for nationals of IN, ID, LA, MM, VN. Rule tw-tac-with-visa in countries/tw.md
- Notes: Income can be shown with a tax certificate, income certificate, or employment or client contract. Insurance must cover death, injury and illness with at least JPY 10 million of medical cover. An activity plan is also required. The eligible-country list sits in an Immigration Services Agency PDF and is not copied here.

### Short-term stay visa - jp-short-term-visa

- Document: visa
- Purpose: tourism, visiting relatives or friends, business
- Fee: About JPY 15,000 (single entry); about JPY 30,000 (multiple entry), paid in local currency
- Waiting time: Standard processing is five working days after the application is accepted
- How to apply: At a Japanese embassy or consulate, or online through JAPAN eVISA where available
- Eligibility: Nationals who are not visa-exempt for short stays
- For ranking: fee about USD 100, 5 days
- Required documents: passport, passport-photo, application-form, flight-booking
