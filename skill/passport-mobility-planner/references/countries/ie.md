## Long-term residence and citizenship routes

- Document: residence-permit
- Purpose: closed investor residence route
- How to apply: Closed to new applications from 15 February 2023
- For ranking: fee unknown, waiting days unknown

## Visas and travel authorisations you can apply for

### Short stay 'C' visa (visit) - ie-short-stay-c-visa

- Document: visa
- Purpose: tourism, visiting family or friends
- Fee: EUR 60 (single entry); EUR 100 (multiple entry)
- Waiting time: About 8 weeks from when the documents arrive at the visa office
- Stay: Up to 90 days
- How to apply: Online through AVATS, then send the passport and documents to the visa office within 30 days
- Eligibility: Visa-required nationals visiting Ireland; work is not allowed
- Proof of funds: Bank statements showing enough money for the trip, plus evidence of ties that require you to return home
- For ranking: fee about USD 66, 56 days
- Required documents: application-form, passport, passport-photo, invitation-letter, accommodation-proof, bank-statements, travel-insurance, employment-letter
- Also unlocks:
  - Georgia: visa-free entry (valid on the day of entry into Georgia). Rule ge-entry-with-visa in countries/ge.md
  - South Korea: visa-free transit (valid visa for one of the 32 listed European countries; travelling via Korea to one of them, or from one of them via Korea to home or a third country). Not for nationals of SY, SD, IR, MK, CU, XK, PS, AF, IQ, NG, GH, YE, GM, SN, BD, KG, PK, SO, UZ, NP, CM, LK, MM, EG. Rule kr-transit-with-european-visa in countries/kr.md
  - Türkiye: e-visa (valid visa; further conditions apply but are not spelled out on the ministry page). Only for nationals of AF, DZ, BD, IN, IQ, LY, NP, PK, SN, YE, CV, EG, GQ, PS, PH, LK. Rule tr-evisa-with-visa in countries/tr.md
- Notes: The fee is not refunded. Some consulates add their own charges. Applicants must disclose any previous visa refusals.
- Source: https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-visit-ireland/how-to-apply-for-a-short-stay-c-visit-tourist-visa/visit-family-friend-visa/ (Immigration Service Delivery, Department of Justice (Ireland))
