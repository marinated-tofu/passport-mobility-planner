### Stopover program - et-ethiopian-addis-ababa-stopover

- Stay: connection of 8 to 24 hours (usually 1 night)
- Purpose: free hotel, meals and transfers during a long connection in Addis Ababa
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - ticket; single Ethiopian Airlines ticket with both flights operated by Ethiopian, connecting in Addis Ababa for 8 to 24 hours
- Notes: Free hotel (usually one night), meals and airport transfers. The voucher is issued automatically at the origin station or at the transit desk in Addis Ababa.
