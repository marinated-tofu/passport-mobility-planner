## Benefits from visas and residence permits you already hold

### Visa-free entry - ba-entry-with-residence

- Stay: 90 days in any 180 days; one continuous stay no longer than 30 days
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by any Schengen state, any EU state, United States; valid residence permit; the stay must fall within its validity
- Notes: Same Decision on Visas rule for residence permits from Schengen states, EU states or the US. UK residence permits no longer count.
- Source: https://granpol.gov.ba/en/faq (Border Police of Bosnia and Herzegovina)

### Visa-free entry - ba-entry-with-visa

- Stay: 90 days in any 180 days; one continuous stay no longer than 30 days
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - visa issued by any Schengen state, any EU state, United States; valid multiple-entry visa; the stay must fall within its validity
- Notes: Under the Decision on Visas, nationals who normally need a Bosnian visa can enter with a passport and a valid multiple-entry visa from a Schengen state, an EU state or the US. UK visas stopped qualifying at the end of the Brexit transition period (31 December 2020).
- Source: https://granpol.gov.ba/en/faq (Border Police of Bosnia and Herzegovina)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified
