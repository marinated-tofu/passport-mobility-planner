## Visas and travel authorisations you can apply for

### International teleworker visa (digital nomad) - es-international-teleworker-visa

- Document: visa
- Purpose: remote work for companies outside Spain
- Validity: Authorises residence and work in Spain for one year
- Stay: One year; a residence authorisation can then be requested in Spain within the last 60 days of the visa
- How to apply: At the Spanish consulate for the place of residence
- Eligibility: Adult non-EU nationals working remotely for companies outside Spain using only computer and telecoms systems; self-employed applicants need a commercial contract of at least 3 months with the foreign company
- Proof of funds: Income from work of at least 200% of the Spanish minimum wage (SMI) per month for the holder (the FAQ states '1,221 euros at present'), plus 75% of the SMI for the first family member and 25% for each additional one
- For ranking: fee unknown, waiting days unknown
- Required documents: passport, application-form, employment-contract, police-clearance, health-insurance, proof-of-income, other
- Notes: Social Security registration is required. Criminal record certificates cover the last 2 years plus a sworn statement for 5 years. Health insurance must be public or private Spanish-authorised cover; travel insurance does not count. The FAQ lists a EUR 73.26 fee for residence authorisations filed inside Spain; the consular visa fee is not given. Professionals may earn up to 20% of their work from Spanish companies.
- Source: https://www.inclusion.gob.es/documents/d/unidadgrandesempresas/digital-nomad-faqs-english (Ministry of Inclusion, Social Security and Migration of Spain (Large Companies and Strategic Groups Unit))
