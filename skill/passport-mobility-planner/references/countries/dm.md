## Long-term residence and citizenship routes

### Dominica CBI - Economic Diversification Fund (EDF) - dm-cbi-economic-diversification-fund

- Document: citizenship
- Purpose: citizenship through investment
- Fee: Processing USD 1,000 per application; due diligence USD 7,500 main applicant, USD 4,000 per dependant 16+; naturalisation certificate USD 500 per person; interview USD 1,000 per person 16+
- Minimum investment: USD 200,000 single applicant; USD 250,000 main applicant plus up to three dependants; USD 25,000 per extra dependant under 18; USD 40,000 per extra dependant 18+
- Stay: Mandatory interview for applicants 16+; no residence period stated
- How to apply: Only through an Authorised Agent
- Eligibility: Must pass due diligence and interview; banned and restricted nationalities apply
- Proof of funds: 12 months of bank statements, bank reference letter, source-of-funds documents and notarised source-of-funds affidavit
- For ranking: fee about USD 10,000, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 200,000
- Required documents: application-form, passport, national-id, passport-photo, birth-certificate, marriage-certificate, police-clearance, medical-certificate, degree-certificate, bank-statements, employment-letter, cv, other
- Not for nationals of: Belarus, Russian Federation, Yemen
- Notes: Non-refundable contribution. Northern Iraq nationals are also banned. North Korea, Sudan and Iran nationals are refused unless they have lived 10+ years elsewhere with no assets or business ties there.
- Source: https://www.cbiu.gov.dm/investment-options/economic-diversification-fund/ (Citizenship by Investment Unit, Commonwealth of Dominica)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### Dominica CBI - Real Estate (Approved Project) - dm-cbi-real-estate

- Document: citizenship
- Purpose: citizenship through property investment
- Fee: Government fees: USD 75,000 main applicant; USD 100,000 main applicant plus up to three dependants; USD 25,000 per extra dependant under 18; USD 40,000 per extra dependant 18+. Due diligence USD 7,500 main, USD 4,000 per dependant 16+
- Minimum investment: USD 200,000 in a unit in an Approved Project
- How to apply: Only through an Authorised Agent
- Eligibility: Same due diligence and nationality restrictions as the EDF route
- Proof of funds: 12 months of bank statements and source-of-funds evidence; sale and purchase agreement
- For ranking: fee about USD 82,500, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 200,000
- Required documents: application-form, passport, passport-photo, birth-certificate, police-clearance, medical-certificate, bank-statements, other
- Not for nationals of: Belarus, Russian Federation, Yemen
- Notes: Hold for 3 years from the citizenship grant, or 5 years if the next buyer is also a CBI applicant. Government fees are due only on approval.
- Source: https://www.cbiu.gov.dm/investment-options/real-estate/ (Citizenship by Investment Unit, Commonwealth of Dominica)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified
