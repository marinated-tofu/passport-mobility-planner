## Airline and airport stopover programs

### Stopover program - sa-saudi-stopover-transit-visa

- Stay: up to 96 hours; visa valid 3 months, single entry
- Purpose: stopover in Saudi Arabia, including tourism and Umrah
- Applies only if every check is yes. Write each check and its answer in your reply; one no means this rule does not apply.
  1. Does the traveller hold a ticket? It must meet: flight booked with Saudia or flynas through Saudi Arabia; the stopover visa is requested during the booking.
- Partial rule: if it applies, tell the traveller to confirm it on the source below.
- Notes (if the rule applies, copy every form to fill and every caveat into the reply): The visa itself has no fee, but there is an application charge of about USD 10.50 plus required medical insurance from about USD 3.45. It is issued through the airline booking, valid 3 months for one entry, with a stay of up to 96 hours. Umrah needs a Nusuk appointment. The page does not say whether the onward flight must go to a third country. A free hotel night reported elsewhere is not on this page and is not included. Page updated 16 Sep 2026.

## Visas and travel authorisations you can apply for

### Tourist eVisa for holders of US, UK or Schengen visas or residence - sa-evisa-us-uk-schengen-visa-holders

- Document: visa
- Purpose: tourism
- Fee: SAR 339.44, medical insurance not included (shown as about USD 90.50)
- Validity: Multiple entry: 1 year. Single entry: 3 months
- Stay: Up to 90 days per stay
- How to apply: Online on the KSA Visa portal
- Eligibility: Nationals not otherwise eligible for the eVisa who hold a valid US, UK or Schengen tourist or business visa, or residence in those countries
- For ranking: fee about USD 90, waiting days unknown
- Required documents: passport, visa, application-form
- Also unlocks:
  - Georgia: visa-free entry (valid on the day of entry into Georgia). Rule ge-entry-with-visa in countries/ge.md
- Notes: The visa must have been used at least once to enter the issuing country, with an entry stamp; digital stamps are not accepted for a first visit. Passport must be valid at least 6 months. Holders of these visas may also get a visa on arrival. Fee checked for a Philippine passport; it can vary by nationality.
- Source: https://www.visitsaudi.com/en/plan-your-trip/visa-regulations (Saudi Tourism Authority)
