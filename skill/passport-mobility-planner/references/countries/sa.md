Holders of these visas may also get a visa on arrival. Fee checked for a Philippine passport; it can vary by nationality.

### Tourist eVisa - sa-tourist-evisa

- Document: visa
- Purpose: tourism, Umrah outside Hajj season, visiting family and friends
- Fee: SAR 395 including medical insurance (shown as about USD 105.32)
- Validity: Multiple entry: 1 year. Single entry: 3 months
- Stay: Up to 90 days per stay
- How to apply: Online on the Saudi eVisa portal
- Eligibility: Nationals of eligible countries; travellers under 18 must be accompanied

- Also unlocks:

- Notes: Passport must be valid at least 6 months beyond entry (U.S. citizens are exempt from this). The fee figure was shown with a currency symbol image and a USD equivalent; it varies by nationality. The page also offers visa on arrival to eligible nationals.
