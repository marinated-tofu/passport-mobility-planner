## Benefits from a Gulf (GCC) residence permit

### Visa-free entry - ge-visa-free-gcc-visa-or-residence

- Stay: 90 days in any 180-day period
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by Qatar, United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman; valid on the day of entry, shown by a travel or other document
- Notes: All six GCC states are on Georgia's list of countries whose visa or residence permit lets a foreigner enter without a visa (a visa from these states also counts). Since the April 2025 amendment, citizens of 17 countries (Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Nigeria and others) face a stricter test; the Georgian embassy in the UAE confirms it for UAE permits (see the next rule). Check that rule before relying on this one.
- Source: https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/2867377?publication=1 (Legislative Herald of Georgia (Government Ordinance No. 256 of 5 June 2015, as amended))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### Visa-free entry - ge-visa-free-uae-residence-restricted-nationalities

- Stay: 90 days in any 180-day period
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by United Arab Emirates; Multiple-entry UAE visa or UAE residence permit valid for at least 1 year on the day of entry to Georgia
- Only for nationals of: Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Cameroon, Congo, Congo, The Democratic Republic of the, Côte d'Ivoire, Ethiopia, Eritrea, Ghana, Morocco, Nigeria, Pakistan, Somalia, Sudan, Syria, Tanzania, Uganda, Yemen
- Notes: Applies to citizens of 17 listed countries. The page says only "Congo", so both Congo states are listed here. If their UAE residence has less than a year left, they need a C1 tourist visa through the Georgian e-visa system. For other nationalities, a UAE permit must be valid on entry and departure and the passport valid at least 90 days.
- Source: https://uae.mfa.gov.ge/visa-information (Embassy of Georgia in the UAE, Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Georgia)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

## Benefits from visas and residence permits you already hold

### Visa-free entry - ge-entry-with-residence

- Stay: 90 days in any 180-day period
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Japan, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland, any Schengen state, Cyprus, Israel, South Korea, United Arab Emirates, Bahrain, Qatar, Oman, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait; valid on the day of entry into Georgia
- Notes: Same ordinance as the visa rule: a residence permit from any listed country also lets the holder enter and stay without a Georgian visa, as long as the permit is valid on the day of entry.
- Source: https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/2867377?publication=1 (Legislative Herald of Georgia (Ministry of Justice), Government of Georgia Ordinance No. 256)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### Visa-free entry - ge-entry-with-visa

- Stay: 90 days in any 180-day period
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - visa issued by United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Japan, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland, any Schengen state, Cyprus, Israel, South Korea, United Arab Emirates, Bahrain, Qatar, Oman, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait; valid on the day of entry into Georgia
- Notes: Government Ordinance No. 256 (2015, last amended by No. 122 of 17 April 2025) lists 50 countries and territories whose visa holders may enter Georgia without a Georgian visa. Besides the main Western issuers and every Schengen state, the list includes Cyprus, Israel, South Korea and the six GCC states, plus some UK, Danish, Dutch and French overseas territories and the Crown Dependencies. The visa only has to be valid when you cross the border.
- Source: https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/2867377?publication=1 (Legislative Herald of Georgia (Ministry of Justice), Government of Georgia Ordinance No. 256)
