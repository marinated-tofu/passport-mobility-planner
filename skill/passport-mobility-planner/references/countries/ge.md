- Notes (if the rule applies, copy every form to fill and every caveat into the reply): Government Ordinance No. 256 (2015, last amended by No. 122 of 17 April 2025) lists 50 countries and territories whose visa holders may enter Georgia without a Georgian visa. Besides the main Western issuers and every Schengen state, the list includes Cyprus, Israel, South Korea and the six GCC states, plus some UK, Danish, Dutch and French overseas territories and the Crown Dependencies.
