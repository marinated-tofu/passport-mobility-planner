## Visas and travel authorisations you can apply for

### Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) - th-destination-thailand-visa

- Document: visa
- Purpose: workcation (digital nomad, remote worker, freelancer), Thai soft power activities such as Muay Thai or cooking courses, medical treatment
- Fee: USD 400 (Royal Thai Embassy, Washington D.C.)
- Validity: 5 years
- How to apply: Online through the Thai e-Visa system (thaievisa.go.th) for the embassy covering your place of residence
- Eligibility: Remote workers, freelancers and digital nomads; participants in Thai soft power activities; spouses and children under 20 of DTV holders
- Proof of funds: Bank statements for the last 3 months with an ending balance of at least THB 500,000 (USD 16,000) each month
- For ranking: fee about USD 400, waiting days unknown
- Required documents: passport, passport-photo, bank-statements, employment-contract, employment-letter, residence-permit, police-clearance
- Notes: Since 31 August 2026 applicants must show proof of permanent residence in the country where they apply (instead of proof of current location) and a criminal record certificate. Passport must be valid 6 months. The fee is set per embassy in local currency, so it differs outside the U.S.
- Source: https://washingtondc.thaiembassy.org/en/page/dtv-visa (Royal Thai Embassy, Washington D.C. (Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Thailand))
