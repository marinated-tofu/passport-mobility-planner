## Visas and travel authorisations you can apply for

### Hayya A1 tourist e-visa - qa-hayya-a1-tourist-visa

- Document: visa
- Purpose: tourism
- Fee: QAR 100, paid after approval
- How to apply: Online through the Hayya platform
- Eligibility: Nationals who do not qualify for a free visa on arrival
- For ranking: fee about USD 27, waiting days unknown
- Required documents: passport, passport-photo, accommodation-proof, flight-booking
- Also unlocks:
  - Georgia: visa-free entry (valid on the day of entry into Georgia). Rule ge-entry-with-visa in countries/ge.md
- Notes: A confirmed hotel booking is required. Passport must be valid at least 3 months. Over 100 nationalities get a free visa on arrival instead. Hayya A2 covers GCC residents. A joint Qatar and Oman tourist visa allows 30 days.
- Source: https://www.visitqatar.com/intl-en/practical-info/visas (Qatar Tourism)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial
