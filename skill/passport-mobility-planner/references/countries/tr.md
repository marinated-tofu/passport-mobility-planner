## Airline and airport stopover programs

### Stopover program - tr-turkish-airlines-stopover-hotel

- Stay: layover of at least 20 hours, up to 7 days
- Purpose: free hotel nights in Istanbul during a connection
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - ticket; Turkish Airlines international-to-international itinerary via Istanbul Airport starting in one of the eligible countries, with a layover of 20 hours or more; request at least 72 hours before the flight
- Notes: Free hotel with breakfast. Economy passengers get 1 night in a 4-star hotel; Business passengers get 2 nights in a 5-star or boutique hotel. Request it through the airline's booking channel or the country email listed on the page, at least 72 hours ahead. Only trips that start in the countries the page lists qualify, and it can be used once per journey (outbound or return). Visa costs are the passenger's. Subject to hotel availability.
- Source: https://www.turkishairlines.com/en-int/flights/stopover/ (Turkish Airlines)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### Stopover program - tr-turkish-airlines-touristanbul

- Stay: 6 to 24 hours layover
- Purpose: free guided city tour during a connection at Istanbul Airport
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - ticket; Turkish Airlines ticket (number starting 235) with an international-to-international connection at Istanbul Airport, both flights on the same booking (PNR), layover between 6 and 24 hours
- Notes: Free. Turkish Airlines runs four tour routes through Istanbul with set departure times. Book online with the PNR or sign up at the Hotel Desk on the Arrivals floor at least 30 minutes before the tour (allow about 45 minutes with passport control). The tour must end at least 1 hour 25 minutes before the next flight. Passengers arrange their own Turkish visa if needed. It cannot be used in the same layover as the Stopover hotel offer. Schedule updated 1 May 2026.
- Source: https://www.turkishairlines.com/en-int/flights/fly-different/touristanbul/ (Turkish Airlines)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

## Benefits from visas and residence permits you already hold

### E-visa - tr-evisa-with-residence

- Stay: single entry; one month (30 days for CV, EG, GQ, PS, PH)
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by any Schengen state, United States, United Kingdom, Ireland; valid residence permit; further conditions apply but are not spelled out on the ministry page
- Only for nationals of: Afghanistan, Algeria, Bangladesh, India, Iraq, Libya, Nepal, Pakistan, Senegal, Yemen, Cabo Verde, Egypt, Equatorial Guinea, Palestine, State of, Philippines, Sri Lanka
- Notes: Same conditional e-visa for these nationalities when they hold a valid Schengen, US, UK or Irish residence permit. Same age and airline limits as the visa rule.
- Source: https://www.mfa.gov.tr/visa-information-for-foreigners.en.mfa (Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Türkiye)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### E-visa - tr-evisa-with-visa

- Stay: single entry; one month (30 days for CV, EG, GQ, PS, PH)
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - visa issued by any Schengen state, United States, United Kingdom, Ireland; valid visa; further conditions apply but are not spelled out on the ministry page
- Only for nationals of: Afghanistan, Algeria, Bangladesh, India, Iraq, Libya, Nepal, Pakistan, Senegal, Yemen, Cabo Verde, Egypt, Equatorial Guinea, Palestine, State of, Philippines, Sri Lanka

## Long-term residence and citizenship routes

### Turkish citizenship by investment (deposit, bonds, fund shares or pension) - tr-citizenship-bank-deposit

- Document: citizenship
- Purpose: citizenship through financial investment
- Minimum investment: USD 500,000 deposited in Turkish banks, or in government bonds, real estate or venture capital fund shares, or private pension, each held at least 3 years
- How to apply: Certified by BDDK (deposits), Ministry of Treasury and Finance (bonds), Capital Markets Board (funds) or SEDDK (pension)
- Eligibility: Granted at the President's discretion
- For ranking: fee unknown, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 500,000
- Required documents: passport, bank-statements, other
- Notes: Amounts follow the 2018 citizenship regulation as amended.
- Source: https://www.invest.gov.tr/en/investmentguide/pages/acquiring-property-and-citizenship.aspx (Presidency of the Republic of Turkiye Investment Office)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### Turkish citizenship by investment (fixed capital or job creation) - tr-citizenship-fixed-capital-or-employment

- Document: citizenship
- Purpose: citizenship through business investment
- Minimum investment: USD 500,000 fixed capital investment, or creating jobs for at least 50 people
- How to apply: Certified by the Ministry of Industry and Technology (capital) or the Ministry of Labor and Social Security (employment)
- Eligibility: Granted at the President's discretion
- For ranking: fee unknown, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 500,000
- Required documents: passport, business-registration, other
- Notes: No holding period stated for these two routes.
- Source: https://www.invest.gov.tr/en/investmentguide/pages/acquiring-property-and-citizenship.aspx (Presidency of the Republic of Turkiye Investment Office)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### Turkish citizenship by investment (real estate) - tr-citizenship-real-estate

- Document: citizenship
- Purpose: citizenship through property investment
- Minimum investment: USD 400,000 in real estate
- How to apply: Register the purchase at the land registry (TKGM) stating it is for citizenship, obtain a certificate of eligibility from the Ministry of Environment, Urbanization and Climate Change, then apply
- Eligibility: Granted at the President's discretion
- For ranking: fee unknown, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 400,000
- Required documents: passport, passport-photo, other
- Notes: The title deed carries a 3-year no-sale annotation. A municipal valuation and earthquake insurance are needed.
- Source: https://www.invest.gov.tr/en/investmentguide/pages/acquiring-property-and-citizenship.aspx (Presidency of the Republic of Turkiye Investment Office)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified
