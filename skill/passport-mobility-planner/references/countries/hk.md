## Visa-free transit

### Visa-free transit - hk-airside-direct-transit-visa-nationals

- Stay: airside only, no entry
- Purpose: direct transit by air without leaving the airport transit area
- Applies only if every check is yes. Write each check and its answer in your reply; one no means this rule does not apply.

Is the traveller a national of none of: Afghanistan, Central African Republic, Congo, The Democratic Republic of the, Côte d'Ivoire, Eritrea, Eswatini, Gambia, Guinea-Bissau, Iran, Iraq, North Korea, Liberia, Libya, Nigeria, Sierra Leone, Somalia, South Sudan, Sudan, Syria, Yemen, Angola, Bangladesh, Burundi, Cameroon, Congo, Ethiopia, Ghana, Rwanda, Sri Lanka, Togo, Pakistan?

Does the traveller hold a passport issued by Azerbaijan, Cambodia, Cuba, Kyrgyzstan, Laos, Lebanon, Moldova, Myanmar, Nepal, Palestine, State of, Senegal, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, Vietnam, Albania, Serbia, Tonga, Tuvalu? It must meet: nationality that needs a visa to visit Hong Kong but is not flagged as needing a visa even airside; for AL and RS this covers the passport types that need a visa (non-biometric ones), for TO and TV the listed passport types; stateless document holders are also covered. Its expiry must be after the last day of the stay; write the expiry date.
  3. Does the traveller hold a ticket? It must meet: onward flight in direct transit, staying inside the airport transit area.
- Notes (if the rule applies, copy every form to fill and every caveat into the reply): Hong Kong's rule is that visa nationals still pass airside without a visa when they connect by air and never leave the transit area. Nationals marked on ImmD's list (the excluded codes here) need a Hong Kong visa even airside; for several of them diplomatic and official passports are exempt. Visitors who do enter must normally hold onward or return tickets unless heading to the Mainland or Macao. List updated August 2026.
