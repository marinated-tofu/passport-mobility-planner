## Visas and travel authorisations you can apply for

### Residence visa for remote work (D8, digital nomad) - pt-d8-digital-nomad-residence-visa

- Document: visa
- Purpose: remote work for employers or clients outside Portugal, leading to residence
- Fee: EUR 110
- Waiting time: Decision within 60 days
- Validity: Valid for 4 months and two entries; the holder then applies to AIMA for a residence permit
- How to apply: At the Portuguese consulate or visa centre for the place of residence
- Eligibility: Employees of foreign employers, or self-employed people with clients abroad, working remotely
- Proof of funds: Average monthly income over the last 3 months of at least 4 times the Portuguese minimum wage (minimum wage EUR 920 in 2026, so EUR 3,680)
- For ranking: fee about USD 121, 60 days
- Required documents: application-form, passport-photo, passport, residence-permit, travel-insurance, police-clearance, employment-contract, business-registration, proof-of-income, tax-return, bank-statements
- Notes: Employees show a work contract or an employer declaration; self-employed applicants show a company contract or service contracts. Passport must be valid 3 months beyond the return date. The criminal record certificate must be apostilled or legalised. Proof of tax residence is required.
- Source: https://vistos.mne.gov.pt/en/national-visas/necessary-documentation/residency (Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Portugal (Consular Affairs))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### Temporary stay visa for remote work (digital nomad) - pt-temporary-stay-digital-nomad-visa

- Document: visa
- Purpose: remote work for employers or clients outside Portugal, short of residence
- Fee: EUR 110

- Eligibility: Employees of foreign employers, or self-employed people with clients abroad, working remotely
