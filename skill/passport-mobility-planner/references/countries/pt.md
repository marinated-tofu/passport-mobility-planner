## Long-term residence and citizenship routes

### ARI residence permit for investment (company capital plus jobs) - pt-ari-company-capital

- Document: residence-permit
- Purpose: residence through founding or capitalising a Portuguese company
- Fee: EUR 842.80 application analysis + EUR 8,418.90 grant (digital rates EUR 632.10 + EUR 6,314.20); renewal EUR 4,210.30
- Minimum investment: EUR 500,000 to set up or recapitalise a company based in Portugal, creating 5 permanent jobs or keeping 10 jobs (5 permanent) for at least 3 years
- Validity: 2 years from issue (initial temporary permit)
- Stay: At least 7 days in the first year and at least 14 days in each following year
- How to apply: Mandatory online pre-registration on the AIMA ARI portal
- Eligibility: Third-country national investing personally or through a company with a permanent establishment in Portugal
- Proof of funds: Bank transfer declaration from a Portuguese-registered credit institution
- For ranking: fee about USD 10,700, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 580,000
- Required documents: passport, visa, health-insurance, police-clearance, application-form, business-registration, employment-contract, other
- Not for nationals of: Portugal, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Poland, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Andorra, Switzerland
- Also unlocks:
  - United Kingdom: visa-free transit (valid common-format residence permit, or a valid common-format category D visa, from an EEA state or Switzerland). Rule gb-airside-twov-eea-swiss-permit-or-d-visa in countries/gb.md
- Notes: Real-estate activity cannot qualify.
- Source: https://aima.gov.pt/pt/viver/autorizacao-de-residencia-para-investimento-art-90-o-a (AIMA - Agencia para a Integracao, Migracoes e Asilo)

## Visas and travel authorisations you can apply for

### Residence visa for remote work (D8, digital nomad) - pt-d8-digital-nomad-residence-visa

- Document: visa
- Purpose: remote work for employers or clients outside Portugal, leading to residence
- Fee: EUR 110
- Waiting time: Decision within 60 days
- Validity: Valid for 4 months and two entries; the holder then applies to AIMA for a residence permit
- How to apply: At the Portuguese consulate or visa centre for the place of residence
- Eligibility: Employees of foreign employers, or self-employed people with clients abroad, working remotely
- Proof of funds: Average monthly income over the last 3 months of at least 4 times the Portuguese minimum wage (minimum wage EUR 920 in 2026, so EUR 3,680)
- For ranking: fee about USD 121, 60 days
- Required documents: application-form, passport-photo, passport, residence-permit, travel-insurance, police-clearance, employment-contract, business-registration, proof-of-income, tax-return, bank-statements
- Notes: Employees show a work contract or an employer declaration; self-employed applicants show a company contract or service contracts. Passport must be valid 3 months beyond the return date. The criminal record certificate must be apostilled or legalised. Proof of tax residence is required.
- Source: https://vistos.mne.gov.pt/en/national-visas/necessary-documentation/residency (Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Portugal (Consular Affairs))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified
