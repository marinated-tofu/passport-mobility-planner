## Visas and travel authorisations you can apply for

### Digital Nomad Visa - ee-digital-nomad-visa

- Document: visa
- Purpose: remote work for employers or clients outside Estonia
- Waiting time: Up to 30 days
- Stay: Up to 1 year
- How to apply: At the nearest Estonian embassy; standard visa rules apply
- Eligibility: Location-independent workers employed by a company registered abroad, running their own foreign company, or freelancing mainly for clients outside Estonia
- Proof of funds: Income of at least EUR 4,500 net per month (as stated on the e-Residency page)
- For ranking: fee unknown, 30 days
- Required documents: passport, application-form, employment-contract, business-registration, proof-of-income
- Notes: Read on the government e-Residency site, not the Police and Border Guard Board or Foreign Ministry pages (those URLs did not resolve). The fee and the gross or net basis of the income rule should be checked with the embassy.
- Source: https://www.e-resident.gov.ee/nomadvisa/ (Republic of Estonia e-Residency programme)
