## Visas and travel authorisations you can apply for

### European Travel Information and Authorisation System (ETIAS) - eu-etias

- Document: electronic travel authorisation
- Purpose: short stays in European countries covered by ETIAS
- Fee: EUR 20
- How to apply: Not open yet; applications will be made online once ETIAS starts
- Eligibility: Visa-exempt non-EU nationals, once the system is in operation
- For ranking: fee about USD 22, waiting days unknown
- Required documents: passport, application-form
- Notes: Not in operation on 2026-11-07 and no applications are being collected. The Commission gives the start as the last quarter of 2026, with the exact date to be announced. The EUR 20 fee was announced on 17 July 2025. The Entry/Exit System has been fully running since 10 April 2026. ### Schengen short-stay visa (type C) - schengen-short-stay-visa-c

- Document: visa
- Purpose: tourism, family visits, business, short stays
- Fee: EUR 90 (adults); EUR 45 (children aged 6 to 12)
- Waiting time: 15 calendar days, extendable up to 45 days in individual cases
- Stay: Up to 90 days in any 180-day period
- How to apply: At the consulate (or its external provider) of the country of main destination, no earlier than 6 months and at least 15 days before travel; fingerprints are taken
- Eligibility: Non-EU nationals who need a visa for short stays in the Schengen area
- Proof of funds: Proof of sufficient means for the stay; each country sets its own reference amount (see funds_reference)
- For ranking: fee about USD 99, 15 days
- Required documents: application-form, passport, passport-photo, travel-insurance, bank-statements, accommodation-proof, flight-booking
- Also unlocks:
  - Albania: visa-free entry (valid multiple-entry visa (electronic formats accepted for EU/US/UK) that has already been used to enter the issuing country or the Schengen area). Not for nationals of IR. Rule al-entry-with-visa in countries/al.md
  - Bosnia and Herzegovina: visa-free entry (valid multiple-entry visa; the stay must fall within its validity). Rule ba-entry-with-visa in countries/ba.md
  - Dominican Republic: visa-free entry (valid multiple-entry visa). Rule do-entry-with-visa in countries/do.md
  - Georgia: visa-free entry (valid on the day of entry into Georgia). Rule ge-entry-with-visa in countries/ge.md
  - North Macedonia: visa-free entry (multiple-entry type C visa, valid at least 5 days beyond the planned stay). Rule mk-entry-with-schengen-visa in countries/mk.md
  - Peru: visa-free entry (visa valid for at least 6 months). Only for nationals of CN, IN. Rule pe-entry-with-visa-cn-in in countries/pe.md
  - Serbia: visa-free entry (valid visa in a national passport (not emergency or convention travel documents)). Rule rs-entry-with-visa in countries/rs.md
  - South Korea: visa-free transit (valid visa for one of the 32 listed European countries; travelling via Korea to one of them, or from one of them via Korea to home or a third country). Not for nationals of SY, SD, IR, MK, CU, XK, PS, AF, IQ, NG, GH, YE, GM, SN, BD, KG, PK, SO, UZ, NP, CM, LK, MM, EG. Rule kr-transit-with-european-visa in countries/kr.md
  - Taiwan: electronic travel authorisation (valid entry visa (electronic visas accepted), or one that expired less than 10 years before arrival; work permits do not count). Only for nationals of IN, ID, LA, MM, VN. Rule tw-tac-with-visa in countries/tw.md
  - Türkiye: e-visa (valid visa; further conditions apply but are not spelled out on the ministry page). Only for nationals of AF, DZ, BD, IN, IQ, LY, NP, PK, SN, YE, CV, EG, GQ, PS, PH, LK. Rule tr-evisa-with-visa in countries/tr.md
- Notes: Lower fees apply under visa facilitation agreements: EUR 35 for nationals of Armenia, Azerbaijan and Belarus, EUR 67.50 for Cabo Verde. Passport must be valid at least 3 months after leaving and have been issued in the last 10 years. Medical insurance must cover emergencies, hospital care and repatriation. Applicants also show they intend to return home.
