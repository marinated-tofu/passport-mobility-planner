- Stay: up to 180 days in a one-year period, in one visit or several
- Purpose: tourism
- Applies only if every check is yes. Write each check and its answer in your reply; one no means this rule does not apply.

  2. Does the traveller hold a visa issued by United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, any Schengen state? It must meet: visa valid for at least 6 months. Work it out: the entry date plus 6 months = (date). The visa's expiry must be on or after that date; write both dates.
