### Visa-free entry - pe-entry-with-visa-cn-in

- Stay: up to 180 days in a one-year period, in one visit or several
- Purpose: tourism
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - visa issued by United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, any Schengen state; visa valid for at least 6 months
- Only for nationals of: China, India
- Notes: Chinese and Indian citizens do not need Peru's temporary tourist visa if they carry a visa from one of these countries with six or more months of validity left. Basis: Supreme Decrees 069-2016-RE and 06-2017-RE.
