## Benefits from visas and residence permits you already hold

### Visa-free entry - mk-entry-with-residence

- Stay: up to 15 days per entry; at most 90 days in any 180 days
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by any EU state, any Schengen state; valid temporary or permanent residence in an EU member state or Schengen country
- Notes: Third-country nationals living in the EU or the Schengen area, whether on temporary or permanent residence, can make visa-free visits of up to 15 days each.
