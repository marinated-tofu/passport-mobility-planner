## Long-term residence and citizenship routes

### EB-5 Immigrant Investor - us-eb5-immigrant-investor

- Document: residence-permit
- Purpose: permanent residence through investment
- Fee: Form I-526 or I-526E: USD 3,675; initial I-526E filings also pay USD 1,000 (EB-5 Reform and Integrity Act); Form I-829: USD 3,750
- Minimum investment: USD 1,050,000 standard; USD 800,000 in a targeted employment area or infrastructure project (petitions filed on or after 15 March 2022)
- Validity: Conditional permanent residence; conditions removed by filing Form I-829
- How to apply: File Form I-526 (standalone investor) or Form I-526E (regional center investor) with USCIS
- Eligibility: Invest the required capital in a new commercial enterprise that creates at least 10 full-time jobs for qualifying US workers; through a regional center, up to 90% may be indirect jobs
- Proof of funds: Lawful source of the invested capital must be shown
- For ranking: fee about USD 4,675, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 800,000
- Required documents: passport, application-form, bank-statements, tax-return, business-registration, other

### B1/B2 visitor visa - us-b1-b2-visa

- Document: visa
- Purpose: tourism, business visits
- Fee: USD 185
- How to apply: Complete the online DS-160 form, pay the fee and attend an interview at a U.S. embassy or consulate (applicants aged 14 to 79 are generally interviewed)
- Eligibility: Applicants who show the purpose of the trip, intent to leave the U.S., and the means to pay for the trip
- Proof of funds: Evidence that you can pay the costs of the trip; the embassy may ask for proof of employment, family ties or finances
- For ranking: fee about USD 185, waiting days unknown
- Required documents: passport, passport-photo, application-form, employment-letter, bank-statements

### ESTA (Electronic System for Travel Authorization) - us-esta

- Document: electronic travel authorisation
- Purpose: tourism, business, transit under the Visa Waiver Program
- Fee: USD 40.62
- Validity: Usually two years, or until the passport expires if sooner
- Stay: 90 days or less per visit under the Visa Waiver Program
- How to apply: Online through the official ESTA website or the ESTA Mobile app before travel
- Eligibility: Citizens of Visa Waiver Program countries travelling for 90 days or less who hold an electronic passport
- For ranking: fee about USD 41, waiting days unknown
- Required documents: passport, application-form
- Notes: The USD 40.62 fee for fiscal year 2027 is set by Federal Register notice 2026-20185 (published 1 October 2026, in force 15 days later): USD 17 processing plus USD 10.62 and USD 13 in other charges. A refused application is charged USD 10.62. CBP gives no processing time on the FAQ page. Notice: https://www.federalregister.gov/d/2026-20185
- Source: https://www.cbp.gov/travel/international-visitors/esta/frequently-asked-questions-about-visa-waiver-program-vwp-and-electronic-system-travel (U.S. Customs and Border Protection)
