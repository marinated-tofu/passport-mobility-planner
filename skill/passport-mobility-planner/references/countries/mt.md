## Long-term residence and citizenship routes

### Citizenship by Naturalisation for Exceptional Services by Direct Investment (discontinued) - mt-naturalisation-exceptional-services-direct-investment

- Document: citizenship
- Purpose: citizenship through investment (ended)
- How to apply: Closed; no longer accepted
- For ranking: fee unknown, waiting days unknown
- Notes: The EU Court of Justice ruled on 29 April 2025 (Case C-181/23) that the scheme breaches EU law. Malta amended its Citizenship Act in July 2025 and ended the route. The remaining merit route is not an investment scheme.
- Source: https://komunita.gov.mt/2025/07/23/press-release-the-government-publishes-amendments-to-the-maltese-citizenship-act/ (Community Malta Agency, Government of Malta)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP) - mt-permanent-residence-programme

- Document: residence-permit
- Purpose: permanent residence through property, contribution and fees
- Fee: EUR 60,000 non-refundable administrative fee + EUR 37,000 government contribution + EUR 7,500 per adult dependant (excluding spouse) + EUR 500 residence card per person; plus EUR 2,000 donation to a local NGO
- Minimum investment: Buy qualifying residential property of at least EUR 375,000, or rent one for at least EUR 14,000 a year; capital assets of EUR 500,000 (EUR 150,000 financial) or EUR 650,000 (EUR 75,000 financial)
- Waiting time: No fixed period stated
- Validity: Permanent residence; residence card valid 5 years
- Stay: No physical residence requirement stated
- How to apply: Through a Residency Malta licensed agent
- Eligibility: Third-country national (not EEA or Swiss), stable and regular resources, clean criminal record, not from a sanctioned country
- Proof of funds: Evidence of capital assets and stable regular income
- For ranking: fee about USD 112,500, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 435,000
- Required documents: passport, health-insurance, police-clearance, other
- Not for nationals of: Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland
- Also unlocks:
  - United Kingdom: visa-free transit (valid common-format residence permit, or a valid common-format category D visa, from an EEA state or Switzerland). Rule gb-airside-twov-eea-swiss-permit-or-d-visa in countries/gb.md
- Notes: Property must be kept 5 years, after which the holder must still keep residential property in Malta. Nationals of sanctioned countries are also excluded (list not given).
- Source: https://residencymalta.gov.mt/legal-framework-mprp-2/ (Residency Malta Agency)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified
