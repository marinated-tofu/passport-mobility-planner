## Long-term residence and citizenship routes

### Antigua and Barbuda CIP - Business Investment - ag-cip-business-investment

- Document: citizenship
- Purpose: citizenship through business investment
- Fee: Processing and due diligence fees as per schedule (USD 10,000 single / USD 20,000 family of up to 4; due diligence USD 8,500 principal)
- Minimum investment: USD 1,500,000 sole investor; or joint investment of at least USD 5,000,000 by at least 2 persons, each at least USD 400,000
- Stay: 5 days in any five-calendar-year period
- How to apply: Through licensed agents or authorised representatives; business must be approved
- Eligibility: Same as the NDF route
- For ranking: fee about USD 18,800, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 1,500,000
- Required documents: application-form, police-clearance, business-registration
- Notes: The fee schedule shows a USD 75,000 spouse fee in one business column, likely an error on the page.
- Source: https://cip.gov.ag/schedule-of-fees/ (Citizenship by Investment Unit, Government of Antigua and Barbuda)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### Antigua and Barbuda CIP - National Development Fund (NDF) - ag-cip-national-development-fund

- Document: citizenship
- Purpose: citizenship through investment
- Fee: Processing USD 10,000 single, USD 20,000 family of up to 4, plus USD 10,000 per dependant from the 5th; due diligence USD 8,500 principal, USD 5,000 spouse, USD 2,000 child 12-17, USD 4,000 dependant 18+; passport USD 300 each
- Minimum investment: USD 230,000 contribution for any family size
- Stay: At least 5 days in Antigua and Barbuda in a five-calendar-year period, or citizenship can be revoked
- How to apply: Through licensed agents or authorised representatives; due diligence and 10% of the processing fee paid on submission
- Eligibility: Principal applicant over 18; excluded for false information, serious disease, unpardoned conviction over 6 months, criminal investigation, security risk, or visa refusal by a visa-free partner country
- For ranking: fee about USD 18,800, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 230,000
- Required documents: application-form, police-clearance
- Notes: Contribution due within 30 days of approval.
- Source: https://cip.gov.ag/schedule-of-fees/ (Citizenship by Investment Unit, Government of Antigua and Barbuda)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### Antigua and Barbuda CIP - Real Estate - ag-cip-real-estate

- Document: citizenship
- Purpose: citizenship through property investment
- Fee: Processing USD 10,000 single, USD 20,000 family of up to 4, plus USD 10,000 per extra dependant; due diligence and passport fees as per schedule
- Minimum investment: USD 300,000 in an approved development
- Stay: 5 days in any five-calendar-year period, or citizenship may be revoked
- How to apply: Through licensed agents or authorised representatives
- Eligibility: Same as the NDF route
- For ranking: fee about USD 18,800, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 300,000
- Required documents: application-form, police-clearance
- Notes: No resale for 5 years unless the buyer moves into another approved property. Ownership through a qualifying local company is allowed.
- Source: https://cip.gov.ag/investment-options/real-estate/ (Citizenship by Investment Unit, Government of Antigua and Barbuda)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified
