## Long-term residence and citizenship routes

### St Kitts and Nevis CBI - Developer's Real Estate Investment - kn-cbi-developer-real-estate

- Document: citizenship
- Purpose: citizenship through property investment
- Fee: Due diligence: USD 10,000 main applicant, USD 7,500 per dependant 16+; post-approval: USD 25,000 main applicant, USD 15,000 spouse, USD 10,000 dependant under 18, USD 15,000 dependant 18+
- Minimum investment: USD 325,000 per main applicant, paid to the developer of an approved development
- Waiting time: 3 to 6 months
- Stay: No residency requirement to keep citizenship
- How to apply: Only through an Authorised Agent
- Eligibility: Interview for main applicant; dependants 16+ may be required
- For ranking: fee about USD 35,000, 180 days, investment about USD 325,000
- Required documents: application-form
- Notes: 7-year holding period. The page FAQ also mentions USD 600,000, which conflicts with the USD 325,000 headline. Insurance fund, conveyance and stamp duty come on top.
- Source: https://ciu.gov.kn/real-estate-investment/ (Citizenship by Investment Unit, Government of St Kitts and Nevis)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### St Kitts and Nevis CBI - Private Real Estate Investment - kn-cbi-private-real-estate

- Document: citizenship
- Purpose: citizenship through property investment
- Fee: Due diligence: USD 10,000 main applicant, USD 7,500 per dependant 16+; post-approval: USD 25,000 main applicant, USD 15,000 spouse, USD 10,000 dependant under 18, USD 15,000 dependant 18+
- Minimum investment: USD 325,000 condominium unit or share in a designated development; USD 600,000 single-family private home
- How to apply: Only through an Authorised Agent; property must be Approved Private Real Estate
- Eligibility: Interview for main applicant; dependants 16+ if deemed necessary
- For ranking: fee about USD 35,000, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 325,000
- Required documents: application-form
- Notes: Property cannot be resold for at least 7 years; an early sale generally disqualifies it for a later CBI application.
- Source: https://ciu.gov.kn/private-real-estate-investment/ (Citizenship by Investment Unit, Government of St Kitts and Nevis)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### St Kitts and Nevis CBI - Public Benefit Option (PBO) - kn-cbi-public-benefit-option

- Document: citizenship
- Purpose: citizenship through investment
- Fee: Due diligence: USD 10,000 main applicant, USD 7,500 per dependant 16+; post-approval fees: spouse USD 15,000, dependant under 18 USD 10,000, dependant 18+ USD 15,000
- Minimum investment: USD 250,000 for main applicant or family of up to four, paid to an Approved Public Benefit Project
- Waiting time: Decision targeted within 120-180 days of acknowledgement
- How to apply: Only through an Authorised Agent
- Eligibility: Interview for main applicant and dependants 16+ where the Unit deems necessary
- For ranking: fee about USD 10,000, 180 days, investment about USD 250,000
- Required documents: application-form
- Notes: The page also gives USD 25,000 / 50,000 figures for additional dependants; how these relate to the post-approval fees is unclear.
- Source: https://ciu.gov.kn/public-benefit-option/ (Citizenship by Investment Unit, Government of St Kitts and Nevis)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### St Kitts and Nevis CBI - Sustainable Island State Contribution (SISC) - kn-cbi-sustainable-island-state-contribution

- Document: citizenship
- Purpose: citizenship through investment
- Fee: Due diligence: USD 10,000 main applicant; USD 7,500 per dependant aged 16+
- Minimum investment: USD 250,000 for main applicant or family of up to four; USD 25,000 per additional dependant under 18; USD 50,000 per additional dependant 18+
- Waiting time: Approval-in-principle, denial or delay notice within 120-180 days of acknowledgement
- Stay: No mandatory travel or residency requirement; main applicant must attend an interview (virtual or in person)
- How to apply: Only through an Authorised Agent on the CIU list
- Eligibility: Interview mandatory for the main applicant; dependants 16+ may be interviewed
- For ranking: fee about USD 10,000, 180 days, investment about USD 250,000
- Required documents: application-form
- Notes: Non-refundable contribution. Further fees are mentioned without amounts; the document list was not checked.
- Source: https://ciu.gov.kn/sustainable-island-state-contribution/ (Citizenship by Investment Unit, Government of St Kitts and Nevis)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial
