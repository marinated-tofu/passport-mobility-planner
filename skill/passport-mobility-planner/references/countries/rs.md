## Benefits from visas and residence permits you already hold

### Visa-free entry - rs-entry-with-residence

- Stay: up to 90 days within six months, and within the permit's validity
- Purpose: entry, transit and stay
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by any Schengen state, any EU state, United States; valid residence permit; card-format permits must be shown with a valid passport
- Notes: Residence permits from Schengen, EU or US authorities also waive the Serbian visa. The UK is not on the residence-permit list, only on the visa list.
- Source: https://mfa.gov.rs/en/citizens/travel-serbia/visa-requirements (Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Serbia)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified

### Visa-free entry - rs-entry-with-visa

- Stay: up to 90 days within six months, and within the visa's validity
- Purpose: entry, transit and stay
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - visa issued by any Schengen state, United Kingdom, any EU state, United States; valid visa in a national passport (not emergency or convention travel documents)
- Notes: Based on a Government decision of 30 October 2014. Any national passport works, but emergency travel documents and convention travel documents do not. Travellers may show two passports if the visa sits in an expired one.
- Source: https://mfa.gov.rs/en/citizens/travel-serbia/visa-requirements (Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Serbia)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified
