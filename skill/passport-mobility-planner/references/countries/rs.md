## Benefits from visas and residence permits you already hold

### Visa-free entry - rs-entry-with-residence

- Stay: up to 90 days within six months, and within the permit's validity
- Purpose: entry, transit and stay
- Applies only if every check is yes. Write each check and its answer in your reply; one no means this rule does not apply.
  1. Does the traveller hold a residence permit issued by any Schengen state, any EU state, United States? It must meet: valid residence permit; card-format permits must be shown with a valid passport. Its expiry must be after the last day of the stay; write the expiry date.
- Notes (if the rule applies, copy every form to fill and every caveat into the reply): Residence permits from Schengen, EU or US authorities also waive the Serbian visa. The UK is not on the residence-permit list, only on the visa list.
- Source: https://mfa.gov.rs/en/citizens/travel-serbia/visa-requirements (Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Serbia)

### Visa-free entry - rs-entry-with-visa

- Stay: up to 90 days within six months, and within the visa's validity
- Purpose: entry, transit and stay
- Applies only if every check is yes. Write each check and its answer in your reply; one no means this rule does not apply.
  1. Does the traveller hold a visa issued by any Schengen state, United Kingdom, any EU state, United States? It must meet: valid visa in a national passport (not emergency or convention travel documents). Its expiry must be after the last day of the stay; write the expiry date.
- Notes (if the rule applies, copy every form to fill and every caveat into the reply): Based on a Government decision of 30 October 2014. Any national passport works, but emergency travel documents and convention travel documents do not. Travellers may show two passports if the visa sits in an expired one.
- Source: https://mfa.gov.rs/en/citizens/travel-serbia/visa-requirements (Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Serbia)
