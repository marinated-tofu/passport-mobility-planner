## Visas and travel authorisations you can apply for

- Document: visa
- Purpose: tourism
- Validity: 5 years, multiple entry
- Stay: Up to 90 days per visit, extendable once by another 90 days
- How to apply: Self-sponsored, through ICP or GDRFA Dubai
- Eligibility: Open to all nationalities
- Proof of funds: Bank balance of USD 4,000 or the equivalent in foreign currency during the last 6 months
- For ranking: fee unknown, waiting days unknown
- Required documents: passport, passport-photo, bank-statements, health-insurance, flight-booking
- Also unlocks:
  - Georgia: visa-free entry (valid on the day of entry into Georgia). Rule ge-entry-with-visa in countries/ge.md
- Notes: Needs UAE-valid health insurance and a return or onward ticket. UAE embassies do not issue tourist visas; shorter tourist visas are arranged through airlines, hotels and travel agents. GCC residents can get a 30-day eVisa. No fee is given on the page.
