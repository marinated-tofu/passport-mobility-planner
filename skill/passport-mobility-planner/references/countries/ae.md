## Long-term residence and citizenship routes

### Golden Visa (investor - public investment / company or deposit) - ae-golden-visa-investor-public-investment

- Document: residence-permit
- Purpose: long-term residence through business or financial investment
- Fee: AED 1,100 residence permit fee + AED 10 Knowledge Dirham + AED 10 Innovation Dirham + AED 20 delivery; AED 500 extra if applying inside the country; issuance fee rises AED 100 for each year over two years
- Minimum investment: Company share of at least AED 2 million, or bank deposit of at least AED 2,000,000 frozen for at least 2 years, or partner in establishments paying at least AED 250,000 tax annually
- Waiting time: 5 days
- Validity: 10 years, extendable if the same conditions are met
- Stay: No minimum stay stated; u.ae says Golden visa holders may stay outside the UAE longer than the usual 6-month limit
- How to apply: GDRFA Dubai smart services (website or app) or Amer service centres; ICP eServices for other emirates
- Eligibility: Investor or partner in a UAE company, depositor, or tax-paying partner; must support self and family without government support
- Proof of funds: Audited financial report from an accredited UAE auditor and company bank statement; for deposits, a bank certificate showing a 2-year freeze; for the tax route, a Federal Tax Authority letter
- For ranking: fee about USD 310, 5 days, investment about USD 545,000
- Required documents: passport, business-registration, bank-statements, tax-return, other

### Multiple-entry tourist visa (5 years) - ae-5-year-multiple-entry-tourist-visa

- Document: visa
- Purpose: tourism
- Validity: 5 years, multiple entry
- Stay: Up to 90 days per visit, extendable once by another 90 days
- How to apply: Self-sponsored, through ICP or GDRFA Dubai
- Eligibility: Open to all nationalities
- Proof of funds: Bank balance of USD 4,000 or the equivalent in foreign currency during the last 6 months
- For ranking: fee unknown, waiting days unknown
- Required documents: passport, passport-photo, bank-statements, health-insurance, flight-booking
- Also unlocks:
  - Georgia: visa-free entry (valid on the day of entry into Georgia). Rule ge-entry-with-visa in countries/ge.md
- Notes: Needs UAE-valid health insurance and a return or onward ticket. UAE embassies do not issue tourist visas; shorter tourist visas are arranged through airlines, hotels and travel agents. GCC residents can get a 30-day eVisa. No fee is given on the page.
