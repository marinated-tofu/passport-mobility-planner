## Visas and travel authorisations you can apply for

### Korea Electronic Travel Authorization (K-ETA) - kr-k-eta

- Document: electronic travel authorisation
- Purpose: tourism, visiting family, events, business meetings
- Fee: KRW 10,000 plus a 3% online payment charge
- Waiting time: Usually within 72 hours
- Validity: 3 years, or until the passport expires if sooner
- Stay: The visa-free period that applies to the traveller's nationality
- How to apply: Online at the official K-ETA website or app, before boarding
- Eligibility: Nationals of visa-free and visa-waiver countries
- For ranking: fee about USD 7, 3 days
- Required documents: passport, passport-photo, application-form
- Notes: The fee is not refunded. Korea has waived K-ETA for some nationalities for set periods; the current exemption list was not checked.
- Source: https://www.k-eta.go.kr/portal/guide/viewetaapplication.do (Ministry of Justice, Republic of Korea (K-ETA Center))
