## Long-term residence and citizenship routes

### Active Investor Plus Visa (Balanced category) - nz-active-investor-plus-balanced

- Document: residence-permit
- Purpose: residence through mixed, lower-risk investment in New Zealand
- Minimum investment: NZD 10 million in acceptable Balanced investments
- Validity: Resident visa; permanent resident visa possible after the investment period
- Stay: 105 days in New Zealand over five years
- How to apply: Apply to Immigration New Zealand
- Eligibility: No English requirement and no age limit for the main applicant; dependent children must be under 24
- Proof of funds: Nominated investment funds of at least NZD 10 million
- For ranking: fee unknown, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 5,800,000
- Required documents: passport, application-form, bank-statements, police-clearance, medical-certificate, other
- Also unlocks:
  - Georgia: visa-free entry (valid on the day of entry into Georgia). Rule ge-entry-with-residence in countries/ge.md
  - Singapore: visa-free transit (long-term pass valid at least 1 month from entry into Singapore). Only for nationals of IN. Rule sg-vftf-with-residence-in in countries/sg.md
  - South Korea: visa-free transit (permanent resident card; travelling via Korea to or from that country). Not for nationals of SY, SD, IR, MK, CU, XK, PS, AF, IQ, NG, GH, YE, GM, SN, BD, KG, PK, SO, UZ, NP, CM, LK, MM, EG. Rule kr-transit-with-us-ca-au-nz-residence in countries/kr.md
  - Taiwan: electronic travel authorisation (valid resident or permanent resident certificate, or one that expired less than 10 years before arrival). Only for nationals of IN, ID, LA, MM, VN. Rule tw-tac-with-residence in countries/tw.md
  - United Kingdom: visa-free transit (valid permanent residence; Canadian PR issued after 28 June 2002; US green card issued after 21 April 1998, or a US immigrant visa with an electronic arrival stamp, or an expired I-551 with a valid I-797 extension letter, or a sealed Form 155A/155B). Rule gb-airside-twov-permanent-residence in countries/gb.md
- Notes: Balanced allows lower-risk assets such as bonds and listed equities. The five-year period was not stated on the page read. Fee not found.
- Source: https://www.investnz.govt.nz/investor-migrants (Invest New Zealand (New Zealand Government))

## Visas and travel authorisations you can apply for

### New Zealand Electronic Travel Authority (NZeTA) - nz-nzeta

- Document: electronic travel authorisation
- Purpose: visits, transit through Auckland Airport
- Fee: From NZD 17
- Waiting time: Allow 72 hours
- Validity: 2 years for travellers (5 years for crew); multiple trips
- Stay: Up to 3 months per visit, or 6 months for UK passport holders; no more than 6 months in any 12 months
- How to apply: Through the NZeTA app or the Immigration New Zealand website
- Eligibility: Citizens of visa waiver countries, cruise passengers, Australian permanent residents, and some Chinese and Pacific Islands Forum citizens travelling from Australia with an eligible Australian visa
- For ranking: fee about USD 10, 3 days
- Required documents: passport, passport-photo
- Notes: Passport must be valid for at least 3 months after the planned departure from New Zealand. The page shows a starting price only; the tourism levy that is usually charged with the NZeTA is not itemised there.
- Source: https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visas/new-zealand-electronic-travel-authority-nzeta/ (Immigration New Zealand, Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified
