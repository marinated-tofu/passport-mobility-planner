Go through the rules in order. Rules for named nationalities come first: when one names the traveller's nationality, it decides, and a general rule after it cannot override its no.

## Visas and travel authorisations you can apply for

### Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA) - ca-eta

- Document: electronic travel authorisation
- Purpose: visits and transit by air
- Fee: CAD 7
- Waiting time: Most approved within minutes; some can take several days
- Validity: Up to five years, or until the passport expires if sooner
- Stay: Usually up to six months at a time
- How to apply: Online on the Government of Canada eTA form before booking the flight
- Eligibility: Visa-exempt foreign nationals flying to, or transiting through, a Canadian airport
- For ranking: fee about USD 5, waiting days unknown
- Required documents: passport, application-form
- Notes: Not needed for arrivals by land or sea. The fee is from the IRCC fee list: https://ircc.canada.ca/english/information/fees/fees.asp
- Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/eta/facts.html (Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC))

### Visitor visa (temporary resident visa) - ca-visitor-visa-trv

- Document: visa
- Purpose: tourism, visiting family, business visits
- Fee: CAD 100 per person (CAD 500 for a family of 5 or more), plus biometrics CAD 85 per person (CAD 170 per family)
- Validity: Up to 10 years, or until the passport or biometrics expire if sooner
- How to apply: Online through IRCC, then give biometrics at a visa application centre
- Eligibility: Nationals who need a visa to visit Canada
- Proof of funds: Show enough money for the stay; IRCC suggests at least 6 months of bank account details
- For ranking: fee about USD 135, waiting days unknown
- Required documents: passport, passport-photo, application-form, bank-statements
