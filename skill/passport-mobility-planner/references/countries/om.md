- Applies only if every check is yes. Write each check and its answer in your reply; one no means this rule does not apply.
  1. Does the traveller hold a residence permit issued by Qatar, United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain? It must meet: main traveller must hold an approved profession. Its expiry must be after the last day of the stay; write the expiry date.

### Visa on arrival - om-visa-gcc-residents-on-arrival

- Stay: 28 days from entry; renewable once for one week
- Purpose: tourism
- Applies only if every check is yes. Write each check and its answer in your reply; one no means this rule does not apply.
  1. Does the traveller hold a residence permit issued by Qatar, United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain? It must meet: Valid GCC residence; profession approved by the Directorate General of Passports and Residence; arriving from the country of residence directly or via another GCC state; passport valid at least 6 months. Work it out: the entry date plus 6 months = (date). The residence permit's expiry must be on or after that date; write both dates.
- Notes (if the rule applies, copy every form to fill and every caveat into the reply): Single entry. Issued at any Omani entry point or at Omani missions in GCC states, with proof of profession. Companions and relatives travelling with the resident can get it too. Fee OMR 5. The page is in Arabic only.
- Source: https://www.rop.gov.om/arabic/Visas.aspx?VisaName=AgccVisa (Royal Oman Police, Sultanate of Oman)

## Visas and travel authorisations you can apply for

### Tourist visit eVisa - om-tourist-evisa

- Document: visa
- Purpose: tourism
- Stay: 10 days, 14 days or one month (single tourist visit); one month per stay on the 1-year multiple-entry visit visa
- How to apply: Online on the Royal Oman Police eVisa portal, at least 4 days before travel
- Eligibility: Nationals of the countries listed on the portal
- For ranking: fee unknown, waiting days unknown
- Required documents: passport, passport-photo, application-form
- Also unlocks:
  - Georgia: visa-free entry (valid on the day of entry into Georgia). Rule ge-entry-with-visa in countries/ge.md
- Notes: Passport must be valid at least 6 months. A joint Oman and Qatar tourist visa also exists. Fees appear only during the application.
- Source: https://evisa.rop.gov.om/en/types-of-visa (Royal Oman Police)
