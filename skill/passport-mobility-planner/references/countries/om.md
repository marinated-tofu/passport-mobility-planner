- Purpose: tourism
- Applies only if every check is yes. Write each check and its answer in your reply; one no means this rule does not apply.
  1. Does the traveller hold a residence permit issued by Qatar, United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain? It must meet: main traveller must hold an approved profession. Its expiry must be after the last day of the stay; write the expiry date.

### Visa on arrival - om-visa-gcc-residents-on-arrival

- Stay: 28 days from entry; renewable once for one week
- Purpose: tourism
- Applies only if every check is yes. Write each check and its answer in your reply; one no means this rule does not apply.
  1. Does the traveller hold a residence permit issued by Qatar, United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain? It must meet: Valid GCC residence; profession approved by the Directorate General of Passports and Residence; arriving from the country of residence directly or via another GCC state; passport valid at least 6 months. Work it out: the entry date plus 6 months = (date). The residence permit's expiry must be on or after that date; write both dates.
- Notes (if the rule applies, copy every form to fill and every caveat into the reply): Single entry. Issued at any Omani entry point or at Omani missions in GCC states, with proof of profession. Companions and relatives travelling with the resident can get it too. Fee OMR 5. The page is in Arabic only.
- Source: https://www.rop.gov.om/arabic/Visas.aspx?VisaName=AgccVisa (Royal Oman Police, Sultanate of Oman)

## Long-term residence and citizenship routes

### Golden Residency (investor) - om-golden-residency-investor

- Document: residence-permit
- Purpose: long-term residence through investment in Oman
- Validity: Up to 10 years
- How to apply: Online via the Golden Residency portal (portal.omanresidence.gov.om)
- Eligibility: Start or invest in a business, own property in tourism zones, buy bonds or listed shares, hold a fixed deposit, employ 50+ Omanis, or be nominated by an invested company
- For ranking: fee unknown, waiting days unknown
- Also unlocks:
  - Armenia: visa-free entry (Valid for at least 6 months from entry; shown as a physical card or passport sticker with title, name, nationality, birth date and validity in Latin script and Gregorian dates). Rule am-temporary-visa-exemption-gcc-residence in countries/am.md
  - Armenia: visa-free entry (valid at least 6 months from the date of entry; physical card or passport sticker showing name, nationality, birth date and validity in Latin script). Rule am-entry-with-residence in countries/am.md
  - Georgia: visa-free entry (valid on the day of entry into Georgia). Rule ge-entry-with-residence in countries/ge.md
  - Georgia: visa-free entry (valid on the day of entry, shown by a travel or other document). Rule ge-visa-free-gcc-visa-or-residence in countries/ge.md
  - Qatar: e-visa (GCC residence valid for 3 months from the application date, and the GCC ID valid at least 3 months from arrival; passport valid at least 3 months from arrival). Rule qa-hayya-a2-gcc-resident-visa in countries/qa.md
  - Qatar: visa on arrival (valid GCC residence permit). Rule qa-visa-on-arrival-gcc-residents in countries/qa.md
  - Saudi Arabia: e-visa (GCC residence visa valid for at least 3 months, in a profession published on visitsaudi.com (per the Ministry of Tourism regulation); passport valid at least 6 months). Rule sa-tourist-evisa-gcc-residents in countries/sa.md
  - United Arab Emirates: e-visa (GCC residence valid for at least 1 year from the arrival date; passport valid at least 6 months from arrival; profession must be on the approved list (assessed by the authority)). Rule ae-evisa-gcc-residents in countries/ae.md
- Notes: The official pages list the routes but no amounts or fees, and the portal could not be read.
- Source: https://omanresidence.gov.om/en-us/ (Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion, Oman)

## Visas and travel authorisations you can apply for

### Tourist visit eVisa - om-tourist-evisa

- Document: visa
- Purpose: tourism
- Stay: 10 days, 14 days or one month (single tourist visit); one month per stay on the 1-year multiple-entry visit visa
- How to apply: Online on the Royal Oman Police eVisa portal, at least 4 days before travel
- Eligibility: Nationals of the countries listed on the portal
- For ranking: fee unknown, waiting days unknown
- Required documents: passport, passport-photo, application-form
- Also unlocks:
  - Georgia: visa-free entry (valid on the day of entry into Georgia). Rule ge-entry-with-visa in countries/ge.md
- Notes: Passport must be valid at least 6 months. A joint Oman and Qatar tourist visa also exists. Fees appear only during the application.
- Source: https://evisa.rop.gov.om/en/types-of-visa (Royal Oman Police)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial
