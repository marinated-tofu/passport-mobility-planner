## Visas and travel authorisations you can apply for

### Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) - gb-eta

- Document: electronic travel authorisation
- Purpose: visits of up to 6 months, transit
- Fee: GBP 20
- Waiting time: Usually a decision within 1 working day; allow up to 3 working days
- Validity: 2 years, or until the passport expires if sooner; multiple trips
- Stay: Up to 6 months per visit
- How to apply: Online through the UK ETA app or GOV.UK
- Eligibility: Nationals who do not need a visa for short UK visits; every traveller needs one, babies and children included
- For ranking: fee about USD 26, 3 days
- Required documents: passport, passport-photo, application-form
- Notes: Paid by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. The fee is not refunded. A photo of the applicant's face is taken or uploaded during the application.
- Source: https://www.gov.uk/eta/apply (UK Visas and Immigration, Home Office (GOV.UK))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, verified
