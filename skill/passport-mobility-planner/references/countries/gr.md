## Long-term residence and citizenship routes

### Golden Visa (residence permit for real estate owners, art. 20B Law 4251/2014) - gr-golden-visa-real-estate

- Document: residence-permit
- Purpose: residence through property ownership
- Fee: EUR 2,000 electronic fee + EUR 16 permit card printing (initial and renewal)
- Eligibility: Third-country national who owns property in Greece (or holds a qualifying hotel lease or timeshare); property must stay owned for renewal
- Proof of funds: Notary certificate on the purchase contract and land registry or cadastre certificate showing no encumbrances
- For ranking: fee about USD 2,340, waiting days unknown
- Required documents: application-form, passport-photo, passport, visa, health-insurance, other
- Also unlocks:
  - United Kingdom: visa-free transit (valid common-format residence permit, or a valid common-format category D visa, from an EEA state or Switzerland). Rule gb-airside-twov-eea-swiss-permit-or-d-visa in countries/gb.md
- Notes: The ministry page mentions EUR 250,000 only for hotel leases and older purchases. It does not list the current area-based thresholds, so the minimum is left blank.
- Source: https://migration.gov.gr/en/golden-visa/ (Ministry of Migration and Asylum, Greece)
