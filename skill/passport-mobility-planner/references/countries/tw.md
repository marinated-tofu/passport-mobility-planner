## Airline and airport stopover programs

### Stopover program - tw-taoyuan-free-half-day-tour

- Stay: layover of 7 to 24 hours
- Purpose: free guided half-day tour during a transit at Taoyuan
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - ticket; transit at Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport with a layover of 7 to 24 hours; book online 5 to 90 days ahead or same day at the airport
  - passport; visa-exempt for Taiwan or holding a valid ROC visa; nationals of IN, ID, MM, VN, KH and LA qualify with a valid Travel Authorization Certificate
- Notes: Free guided tours run by Taiwan's tourism authority, one tour per stopover. A self-guided option gives free Airport MRT tickets to non-Taiwanese travellers entering for up to 24 hours.
- Source: https://halfdaytour.taiwan.net.tw/ (Tourism Administration, Ministry of Transportation and Communications (Taiwan))

## Benefits from visas and residence permits you already hold

### Electronic travel authorisation - tw-tac-with-residence

- Stay: 14 days per visit, starting the day after arrival; certificate valid 90 days, multiple entries
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by United States, Canada, United Kingdom, any EU state, any Schengen state, New Zealand, Australia, Japan, South Korea; valid resident or permanent resident certificate, or one that expired less than 10 years before arrival
- Only for nationals of: India, Indonesia, Laos, Myanmar, Vietnam
- Notes: Same TAC route using a residence document instead of a visa. Vietnamese citizens cannot rely on Japanese or Korean residence documents. Same caveat about older pages listing more nationalities and a longer stay.
- Source: https://www.roc-taiwan.org/ca_en/post/8217.html (Taipei Economic and Cultural Office in Canada, Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Republic of China (Taiwan))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### Electronic travel authorisation - tw-tac-with-visa

- Stay: 14 days per visit, starting the day after arrival; certificate valid 90 days, multiple entries
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - visa issued by United States, Canada, United Kingdom, any EU state, any Schengen state, New Zealand, Australia, Japan, South Korea; valid entry visa (electronic visas accepted), or one that expired less than 10 years before arrival; work permits do not count
- Only for nationals of: India, Indonesia, Laos, Myanmar, Vietnam
- Notes: Citizens of the listed countries can apply online for a free Travel Authorization Certificate (TAC) instead of a visa. Other basics: passport valid six months, onward ticket, no past work in Taiwan as a migrant worker, no immigration violations. Vietnamese citizens cannot use Japanese or Korean documents; others using a Japanese or Korean visa must show they actually entered that country. Australian or New Zealand e-visas must still be valid when entering Taiwan. Maximum six TAC entries per calendar year. An older Bureau of Consular Affairs page still lists the Philippines and Cambodia and a 30-day stay, so the nationality list and stay length should be re-checked.
- Source: https://www.roc-taiwan.org/ca_en/post/8217.html (Taipei Economic and Cultural Office in Canada, Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Republic of China (Taiwan))
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial
