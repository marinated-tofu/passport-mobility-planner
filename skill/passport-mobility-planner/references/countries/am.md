## Benefits from a Gulf (GCC) residence permit

### Visa-free entry - am-temporary-visa-exemption-gcc-residence

- Stay: up to 180 days within one year
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - residence permit issued by Qatar, United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman; Valid for at least 6 months from entry; shown as a physical card or passport sticker with title, name, nationality, birth date and validity in Latin script and Gregorian dates
- Notes: Temporary exemption from 1 July 2026 to 1 July 2027. It covers nationals of 111 countries listed in a PDF linked from the page, which was not opened, so check that list for your nationality. US, EU and Schengen residence permits also qualify.
