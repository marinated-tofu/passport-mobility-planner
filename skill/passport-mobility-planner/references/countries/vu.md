## Long-term residence and citizenship routes

### Vanuatu Capital Investment Immigration Plan - vu-citizenship-capital-investment-immigration-plan

- Document: citizenship
- Purpose: citizenship through investment
- Fee: Application fee USD 250 per application
- Minimum investment: Citizenship fee USD 260,000 covering applicant, spouse and one child under 18; USD 19,250 per extra child under 18; USD 44,250 per dependant aged 18-21
- How to apply: Through Designated Agents
- For ranking: fee about USD 250, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 260,000
- Required documents: application-form
- Notes: The citizenship fee is paid only after Commission approval. The EU visa-waiver suspension applies to all Vanuatu passports.
- Source: https://vancitizenship.gov.vu/index.php/citizenship/fees-and-charges (Vanuatu Citizenship Office and Commission)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### Vanuatu Development Support Program (honorary citizenship) - vu-citizenship-development-support-program

- Document: citizenship
- Purpose: citizenship through investment
- Fee: Application fee VUV 5,000 per application; a USD 5,000 line appears in the fee table without a label
- Minimum investment: USD 130,000 single applicant; USD 150,000 married couple; USD 165,000 couple + 1 child; USD 180,000 couple + 2 children; USD 10,000 per additional applicant
- How to apply: Through Designated Agents listed by the Citizenship Commission
- Eligibility: Subject to Financial Intelligence Unit due diligence
- For ranking: fee about USD 5,000, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 130,000
- Required documents: application-form
- Notes: The fee table is not labelled by programme, so tying these amounts to this programme is an inference. A Vanuatu immigration notice says the EU fully suspended visa-free entry for Vanuatu citizens because of investor citizenship.
- Source: https://vancitizenship.gov.vu/index.php/citizenship/fees-and-charges (Vanuatu Citizenship Office and Commission)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial
