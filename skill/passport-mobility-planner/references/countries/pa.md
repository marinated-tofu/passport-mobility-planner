## Long-term residence and citizenship routes

### Residence for nationals of specific friendly countries (fixed-term deposit) - pa-friendly-nations-fixed-deposit

- Document: residence-permit
- Purpose: residence through a bank deposit
- Fee: Certified cheques: B/. 250.00 to the National Treasury and B/. 800.00 to the National Migration Service
- Minimum investment: Fixed-term deposit of 200,000.00 in a general-licence Panamanian bank, free of liens, for 3 years
- Validity: Provisional residence for 2 years, then permanent residence
- How to apply: File with the Servicio Nacional de Migracion through a notarised power of attorney and application
- Eligibility: Nationals of countries listed in Executive Decree 226 of 2021
- Proof of funds: Bank certificate confirming the deposit amount and 3-year term
- For ranking: fee about USD 1,050, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 200,000
- Required documents: application-form, passport, passport-photo, police-clearance, medical-certificate, bank-statements, other
- Notes: The deposit may be held personally, through a company the applicant ultimately owns, or through a private foundation.
- Source: https://www.migracion.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/LE2.pdf (Servicio Nacional de Migracion, Ministerio de Seguridad Publica, Panama)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial

### Residence for nationals of specific friendly countries (real estate) - pa-friendly-nations-real-estate

- Document: residence-permit
- Purpose: residence through property investment
- Fee: Certified cheques: B/. 250.00 to the National Treasury and B/. 800.00 to the National Migration Service
- Minimum investment: Real estate worth at least 200,000.00, held personally or through a company or private foundation the applicant ultimately owns; may be financed by a local bank
- Validity: Provisional residence for 2 years, then permanent residence
- How to apply: File with the Servicio Nacional de Migracion through a notarised power of attorney and application
- Eligibility: Nationals of countries listed in Executive Decree 226 of 20 July 2021
- Proof of funds: Public Registry certificate proving property ownership
- For ranking: fee about USD 1,050, waiting days unknown, investment about USD 200,000
- Required documents: application-form, passport, passport-photo, police-clearance, medical-certificate, other
- Notes: The same permit can rest on a job offer or a fixed-term deposit. The eligible country list is in the decree, not the requirements sheet, so nationality_only is left empty.
- Source: https://www.migracion.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/LE2.pdf (Servicio Nacional de Migracion, Ministerio de Seguridad Publica, Panama)
- Checked: 07 Nov 2026, partial
