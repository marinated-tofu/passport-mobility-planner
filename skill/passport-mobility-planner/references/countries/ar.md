### Electronic travel authorisation - ar-ave-with-us-visa

- Stay: 3 months from first entry, multiple entries; extendable once by the same period
- Purpose: tourism
- Unlocked by, all of:
  - visa issued by United States; valid B1, B2, J, O, P (P1-P3), E or H-1B visa, valid through the AVE processing time and the planned stay
- Only for nationals of: Albania, Angola, Antigua and Barbuda, Saudi Arabia, Bahamas, Bahrain, Belize, Benin, Botswana, Brunei Darussalam, Burkina Faso, Burundi, Bhutan, Cabo Verde, Cambodia, Cameroon, Côte d'Ivoire, Cuba, Eritrea, Eswatini, Ethiopia, Philippines, Gabon, Gambia, Ghana, Guinea, Equatorial Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Indonesia, Marshall Islands, Solomon Islands, Kenya, Kiribati, Kuwait, Laos, Lesotho, Madagascar, Malawi, Maldives, Mauritius, Moldova, Mozambique, Myanmar, Namibia, Nauru, Nepal, Niger, Oman, Palau, Papua New Guinea, Central African Republic, Congo, Congo, The Democratic Republic of the, Rwanda, Samoa, Sao Tome and Principe, Senegal, Seychelles, Sierra Leone, Taiwan, Tanzania, Togo, Tonga, Tuvalu, Uganda, Vanuatu, Vietnam, Djibouti, Zambia, Zimbabwe
- Notes: The Electronic Travel Authorization (AVE) replaces a consular tourist visa for ordinary-passport holders of the listed nationalities who hold a qualifying US visa. The AVE is valid for 3 months from issue, and the migration office aims to decide within 20 working days. A fee applies.
- Source: https://www.migraciones.gob.ar/ave/index.htm (National Directorate of Migration (DNM), Argentina)
