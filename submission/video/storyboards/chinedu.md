# Demo video storyboard: Chinedu plans London

About 115 seconds at the placeholder timings (likely closer to 105 once Kokoro sets the real ones), 1920×1080, narrated. Every screen in the video shows what the Skill really did in eval run `evals/results/2026-11-11-1214/trip-weak-ng-london.md` (Composer 2.5, mock NAS), shortened. That run scored 4/5. It missed one rubric item: it never confirmed that the date of birth on the UK visa matches the passport (it matched the names only). The video shows this miss in a red note in scene 6 and says it in the narration. Chinedu and his documents are the synthetic household in `evals/households/weak-ng.yaml`. The video says so on screen.

## Who he is

Chinedu Okafor, an electrical engineer at Lekki Grid Energy in Lagos. He has a Nigerian passport and a UK visitor visa (multiple entry, valid 1 Feb 2026 to 31 Jan 2028). His scans sit in `Personal/Travel` on the family NAS: a phone photo of his passport, the UK visa, and an employment letter from October 2026. Text recognition misread one character of the passport number in the photo's machine readable zone (a letter O for a zero).

He is going to London, 3 to 17 March 2027. The cheapest flights from Lagos change planes in Amsterdam or Paris; the direct one costs more. He wants to know which to book and what else he needs. He won't upload passport scans to a cloud chatbot.

## Notes

- One narrator voice (Kokoro `af_heart`). Scene lengths follow the narration; the durations file is a placeholder until `narrate.py` runs.
- The chat window is a plain mock-up labelled "NAS assistant", not UGREEN's real interface.
- The household has only three files, so scene 2 shows three document cards, not eight.
- The answer gives no fee or source for the UK visa beyond "none new" and GOV.UK, so the rule card is the Schengen visa, the one rule that decides the routing.
- The plan's Schengen deadline keeps the answer's "~10 Feb 2027" as written.
- Colours: night navy, cream, a grey London sky; green for met, amber for missing, red for what the run missed.
